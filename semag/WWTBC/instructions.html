🎯 About the Game

Who Wants To Be A CA is a free educational quiz game developed by Calcium Notes for Chartered Accountancy students. The objective of the game is to make revision engaging by combining conceptual MCQs with an interactive game format.

Unlike traditional MCQ practice, every game generates a fresh combination of questions from the question bank. Questions become progressively more challenging as you advance through the game, encouraging students to think, analyse and apply concepts instead of relying solely on memorisation.

The game is suitable for students preparing for ICAI examinations as well as anyone wishing to strengthen their understanding of accounting, auditing, taxation, law and finance.

🕹️ How to Play

The game is designed to be simple and intuitive. Follow the steps below to begin your challenge.

  1. Read these instructions carefully before starting.
  2. Click the Start Game button.
  3. A new game with randomly selected questions will be generated.
  4. Read each question carefully before selecting an answer.
  5. Click on one option to select it.
  6. Once you are confident, click Lock Answer.
  7. The answer will be evaluated immediately.
  8. Read the explanation to understand the concept.
  9. Click Next Question to continue.
  10. Repeat until all questions are completed or the timer expires.

❓ Question Pattern

Every game consists of 15 multiple-choice questions. Questions are selected randomly from the question bank, ensuring that each playthrough feels different.

Each question contains:

  • One question
  • Four answer options
  • Only one correct answer
  • Detailed explanation after answering

Questions are selected intelligently to provide a balanced mix of easy, moderate and challenging concepts.

⏱️ Time Limit

Every question comes with an individual countdown timer.

  • Each question starts with 30 seconds.
  • The timer begins automatically when the question appears.
  • If the timer reaches zero, the question is treated as unanswered.
  • The correct answer and explanation will be displayed automatically.
  • You may then proceed to the next question.
Important:

Managing your time is just as important as knowing the correct answer. Avoid spending too much time on a single question.

🧠 Random Question Selection

Questions are not presented in a fixed order. Instead, the game creates a new quiz every time you play.

  • Questions are selected randomly.
  • Duplicate questions are avoided within the same game.
  • The difficulty progression remains balanced.
  • Different subjects may appear in different combinations.
  • Replaying the game provides a fresh learning experience.
Because the question bank is continuously expanding, every visit offers an opportunity to discover new concepts and strengthen your understanding.

🛟 Lifelines

To make the game more strategic and enjoyable, you are provided with three special lifelines. Each lifeline can be used only once per game.

Lifeline Description
50 : 50 Removes two incorrect options, leaving one correct answer and one incorrect answer.
+30 Seconds Adds an extra 30 seconds to the timer for the current question.
Change Question Replaces the current question with another question of a similar difficulty level.
Use your lifelines wisely. Once a lifeline has been used, it cannot be used again during the same game.

🏅 Scoring System

Your score is calculated based on the number of correct answers. There is no negative marking, so always attempt every question.

Correct Answer +2 Marks
Incorrect Answer 0 Marks

The maximum possible score is based on the total number of questions in the game.

💡 Tips for Success

  • Read every question carefully before selecting an answer.
  • Do not rush through conceptual questions.
  • Use the 50:50 lifeline only when genuinely unsure.
  • Save the +30 Seconds lifeline for lengthy or calculation-based questions.
  • Use Change Question only when you are completely unfamiliar with a topic.
  • Pay close attention to the explanation after every question—it is an important part of the learning process.
  • Replay the game regularly to encounter new questions and reinforce concepts.
  • Aim to improve your score with each attempt rather than focusing only on completing the game.
Consistent practice with randomized questions helps strengthen conceptual understanding and improves confidence for examinations.

⚖️ Fair Play Policy

The purpose of Who Wants To Be A CA is to promote learning and self-assessment. To get the maximum benefit from the game, we encourage every player to play honestly.

  • Do not search for answers while the timer is running.
  • Do not use AI tools or external websites during gameplay.
  • Avoid discussing questions with others until you have completed the game.
  • Treat every game as a real examination.
  • Focus on improving your concepts rather than simply achieving a high score.
The more honestly you play, the more accurately the game reflects your actual preparation level.

📖 Learning Philosophy

Learning is not about getting every answer correct on the first attempt. It is about understanding concepts, identifying mistakes, and continuously improving.

Each question has been selected to encourage analytical thinking, conceptual clarity, and practical application. Whether you answer correctly or incorrectly, take a moment to read the explanation before moving on.

Every game is another opportunity to strengthen your knowledge and build confidence for professional examinations.
