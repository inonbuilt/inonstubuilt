🎯 About CA Final Stress Buster

Preparing for Chartered Accountancy (CA Foundation, CA Inter, and CA Final) demands intensive concentration and marathon revision sessions. Staring at legal sections, tax provisions, and auditing standards for 8 to 12 hours a day leads to cognitive fatigue.

CA Final Stress Buster was crafted specifically for CA aspirants as a quick, therapeutic outlet. Powered by the Matter.js 2D physics engine, the game converts dreaded subjects, revision test papers (RTPs), mock test papers (MTPs), penalties, and amendments into tangible, destructible physics objects that you can bat, smash, collide, and pulverize into colorful dust particles.

💡 Study Break Science: Research shows that short, active micro-breaks (5–10 minutes) involving tactile visual feedback reset mental clarity and boost subsequent retention far better than passive scrolling on social media.

🕹️ How to Play & Review Steps

Follow this simple step-by-step walkthrough to get the most out of your Stress Buster session:

1
Select Your Mode

Choose between 🏏 Bat Mode to swing a cricket bat at incoming subject blocks with mouse speed, or 🖐️ Hand Mode to grab, toss, and fling blocks against the arena walls.

2
Rain Down Blocks

Blocks spawn automatically from the ceiling every 1.5 seconds. Want more mayhem? Click the green 🧱 Drop Blocks button to drop a triple cluster of subject blocks on demand.

3
Deliver Heavy Impacts

In Bat Mode, move your mouse towards a block and click to execute a power swing. The faster your swing and the higher your bat multiplier, the further blocks fly across the screen!

4
Progressive Destruction

Each block takes 3 hits to destroy. Watch them progress through realistic crack overlays and impact flashes before exploding into 40 matching powder particles with screen shake!

5
Tune World Physics

Open ⚙️ Settings to toggle between zero-gravity floatation and hyper-gravity, adjust bat strike force up to 3.0x, and change block sizes from tiny bricks to giants.

6
Zen Immersive Mode

Click 👁️ Hide UI to eliminate all interface distractions and enter a full-screen destress zone. A subtle "Show UI" pill remains accessible in the top-left whenever you need it.

💥 3-Stage Damage & Powder Mechanics

Every subject block in the arena features real-time dynamic damage states and particle physics:

⚡ Hit 1: Hairline Fractures

Impact flash illuminates the block, activating SVG hairline crack textures while the block dims slightly.

⚡ Hit 2: Deep Fissures

Heavy secondary fracture overlays appear. The block darkens dramatically to indicate imminent structural failure.

💥 Hit 3: Powder Shatter

The block completely vaporizes into 40 distinct physics powder particles that burst outward with authentic velocity and screen shake!
