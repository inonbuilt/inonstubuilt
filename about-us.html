🚪 Live Study Rooms: Public vs. Private

Choose the exact level of access, security, and visibility your study session needs.

📡
🌐 Live Public Lobby

Public Study Rooms

Open virtual library rooms broadcasted globally in real time via lightweight MQTT relays so fellow CA aspirants can discover and co-study with you.

  • Open Rooms (🔓 Open): Direct 1-click "🚀 Join Room" entry without passwords. Ideal for library-style peer pressure, late-night focus, and group Pomodoro sprints.
  • Protected Rooms (🔒 Protected): Visible in the lobby to signal an active study session while providing the host complete gatekeeper control.
  • 🔑 Password Access: Enter the host's password directly in the lobby card with smooth typing protection and zero focus interruption.
  • 🙋 "Ask to Join" (Knock & Admit): Guests without passwords tap to knock; the host instantly receives a floating Cyber-Glass popup with bell chime to "✅ Admit" or "✕ Decline".
  • Live Counter & Auto-Hygiene: Real-time member counters display active students; offline rooms automatically prune when the host leaves.
⚡ Best For: Spontaneous study sessions, library ambiance & breaking isolation
🛡️
🕶️ Stealth & Invite-Only

Private Study Rooms

Unlisted, confidential study pods created for close friends, test-series partners, batchmates, and articleship peers who want distraction-free privacy.

  • Zero Public Broadcast: Completely unlisted. Hidden from the public lobby directory; impossible to find without the unique code.
  • Unique 4-Digit Codes (e.g. CA-4829): Ultra-compact room codes designed for quick typing, verbal sharing, or fast room switching.
  • 1-Click WhatsApp & Direct Invites: Share pre-formatted invite links with optional prefilled credentials for instantaneous batchmate onboarding.
  • Optional Host Password: Add a secondary password gate so even if someone enters the room number, they cannot enter without host clearance.
  • Direct P2P Encrypted WebRTC: Video, audio, whiteboard, and timers stream directly peer-to-peer. Zero server transit and zero video cloud recording.
⚡ Best For: Close study circles, coaching batchmates, articleship peers & mock exams
