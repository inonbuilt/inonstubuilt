CA FINAL STUDY MATERIAL & ICAI NEW SCHEME SYLLABUS

Under the ICAI New Scheme of Education and Training (2026-2027), the CA Final curriculum has been streamlined to 6 core papers divided evenly into Group I and Group II. Former Paper 4 (Corporate & Economic Laws) and Paper 5 (Strategic Cost Management & Performance Evaluation) have transitioned into mandatory Self-Paced Online Modules (SPOM Sets A & B), allowing students to concentrate on technical mastery and multidisciplinary practical problem-solving.

GROUP I PAPERS OVERVIEW

Group I focuses on advanced financial reporting, strategic corporate finance, and assurance standards:

  • Paper 1: Financial Reporting (FR) Comprehensive coverage of Ind AS, consolidated financial statements, business combinations, financial instruments (Ind AS 109), revenue recognition (Ind AS 115), and lease accounting (Ind AS 116).
  • Paper 2: Advanced Financial Management (AFM) In-depth mastery of security valuation, portfolio management, foreign exchange risk, interest rate derivatives, advanced capital budgeting, and corporate restructuring.
  • Paper 3: Advanced Auditing & Professional Ethics Extensive preparation for Standards on Auditing (SAs), code of ethics under the Chartered Accountants Act 1949, CARO 2020, forensic audit, digital auditing, and bank audit procedures.

GROUP II PAPERS OVERVIEW

Group II emphasizes direct and indirect taxation, international treaties, and integrated case-based applications:

  • Paper 4: Direct Tax Laws & International Taxation (DT) Corporate tax computation, minimum alternate tax (MAT), assessment proceedings, transfer pricing methods, Double Taxation Avoidance Agreements (DTAA), Model Tax Conventions, and BEPS action plans.
  • Paper 5: Indirect Tax Laws (IDT) Detailed analysis of the Central & Integrated Goods and Services Tax (GST) Acts, input tax credit (ITC) rules, valuation mechanisms, customs laws, assessment procedures, and Foreign Trade Policy.
  • Paper 6: Integrated Business Solutions (IBS) A multidisciplinary, open-book examination integrating concepts from FR, AFM, Audit, Direct Tax, Indirect Tax, Corporate Law, and Strategic Management through real-world business case scenarios.

EXAMINATION PASSING CRITERIA & SPOM ELIGIBILITY

To qualify the CA Final examination under the New Scheme:

  • Subject Minimum: A candidate must secure a minimum of 40% marks in each individual paper.
  • Aggregate Requirement: A minimum of 50% aggregate marks is required across all papers of the respective Group (or both Groups if appearing together).
  • SPOM Mandatory Clearance: Students must qualify SPOM Set A (Corporate Laws) and Set B (Costing & Performance Evaluation) prior to appearing for the CA Final examination.
  • 70:30 Pattern: Core technical papers feature a 30% case-scenario MCQ assessment with no negative marking, alongside 70% descriptive examination questions.
