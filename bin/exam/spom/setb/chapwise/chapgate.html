Official Syllabus Weightage

  • Module 1: Strategic Cost Management (~50 Marks): Lean Systems, Modern Business, Specialist Costing & Emerging Business Models.
  • Module 2: Strategic Performance Management (~50 Marks): Transfer Pricing, Standard Costing, Performance Frameworks & Case Studies.

Phase 1: High-ROI Lockdown

Since the passing threshold is 50% with zero negative marking, attack the highest-yield, calculation-sound chapters first to build a solid 45+ score baseline.


  • Target 1: Lean System & Innovation (Kaizen, 5S, TPM, OEE, JIT, Cellular Mfg).
  • Target 2: Divisional Transfer Pricing & Standard Costing (Formula clarity).
  • Target 3: Performance Measures (Balanced Scorecard, Building Block Model, EVA).

Master "Formulas & Rules" (MCQ Traps)

ICAI constructs case scenarios heavily around qualitative trade-offs and numerical decision boundaries. Keep concise reference points for:


  • Transfer Pricing: Minimum TP = Additional Outlay Cost + Opportunity Cost. Max TP = Market Price.
  • Throughput Accounting: TA Ratio = Throughput per Bottleneck Minute / Factory Cost per Bottleneck Minute.
  • Variances: Planning vs Operational Variances, Market Size vs Market Share.
