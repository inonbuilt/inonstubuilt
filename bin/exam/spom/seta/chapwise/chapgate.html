📊 The Set A Blueprint Strategy

Official Syllabus Weightage

  • Company Law (50 Marks): Directors Core (~20M), Operational Rules (~15M), Winding Up & Misc (~15M).
  • Securities Laws (20 Marks): SEBI Act, LODR, ICDR, SAST, and Insider Trading (PIT).
  • Economic Laws (30 Marks): FEMA (~12M), IBC (~12M), and FCRA (~8M).

Phase 1: High-ROI Lockdown

Since the passing threshold is 50% with zero negative marking, attack the highest-yield, linear chapters first to secure an easy 40–45 marks baseline.


  • Target 1: Economic Laws (IBC & FCRA first).
  • Target 2: The Directors Trio (Appointment, Remuneration, Board Meetings).

Master "The Limits" (MCQ Traps)

ICAI constructs case scenarios heavily around exemptions and numerical thresholds. Keep a single-sheet cheat note specifically for:


  • Time Limits: Days to file forms, hold meetings, or move CIRP applications.
  • Monetary Thresholds: Turnover/Net Worth triggers for Committee compositions (Audit, NRC, SRC).
