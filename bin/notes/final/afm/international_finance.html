1. Master Formula & Parity Cheat Sheet

1. Foreign Risk-Adjusted Discount Rate Formula (Fisher / Parity Relationship):
$$(1 + r_{\text{dom, risk-adjusted}}) = (1 + r_{\text{dom, rf}}) \times (1 + \text{Risk Premium})$$ $$1 + \text{Risk Premium} = \frac{1 + r_{\text{dom, risk-adjusted}}}{1 + r_{\text{dom, rf}}}$$ $$(1 + r_{\text{for, risk-adjusted}}) = (1 + r_{\text{for, rf}}) \times (1 + \text{Risk Premium})$$
2. Purchasing Power Parity (PPP) / Interest Rate Parity (IRP) for Future Exchange Rates:
$$F_t = S_0 \times \left( \frac{1 + I_{\text{foreign}}}{1 + I_{\text{domestic}}} \right)^t \quad \text{or} \quad F_t = S_0 \times \left( \frac{1 + r_{\text{foreign}}}{1 + r_{\text{domestic}}} \right)^t$$
3. Real to Nominal Cash Flow Adjustment:
$$\text{Nominal Cash Flow}_t = \text{Real Cash Flow}_t \times (1 + \text{Inflation Rate})^t$$
4. Modified Internal Rate of Return (MIRR):
$$\text{MIRR} = \left( \frac{\text{Terminal Inflow at Year } n}{\text{Initial Outlay at Year } 0} \right)^{\frac{1}{n}} - 1$$ Where Terminal Inflow = $\sum \left( \text{Cash Inflow}_t \times (1 + k)^{n-t} \right)$.
5. GDR Issue Share Requirement:
$$\text{GDR Issue Price} = \text{Domestic Market Price} \times (1 - \text{Discount}) \times \frac{\text{Domestic Currency}}{\text{Foreign Currency Rate}}$$ $$\text{Number of Shares per GDR} = \text{Fixed Ratio (e.g., 2 shares = 1 GDR)}$$ $$\text{Total Underlying Shares} = \text{Total GDRs Issued} \times \text{Shares per GDR}$$

2. Home Currency vs Foreign Currency Approach

Aspect Home Currency Approach Foreign Currency Approach
Step 1 Forecast cash flows in foreign currency. Forecast cash flows in foreign currency.
Step 2 Convert foreign CFs to home currency using expected forward rates (PPP/IRP). Derive foreign risk-adjusted discount rate using Fisher parity.
Step 3 Discount converted home currency CFs at the domestic risk-adjusted discount rate. Discount foreign CFs directly at foreign risk-adjusted rate to find Foreign NPV.
Step 4 Net Present Value in Home Currency obtained directly. Convert Foreign NPV to Home Currency at the current spot exchange rate ($S_0$).
Theoretical Equivalence Both approaches yield identical NPV if IRP/PPP holds strictly without market imperfections.

3. Parent vs Subsidiary Cash Flow Perspective

Adjustment Item Subsidiary Perspective Parent Perspective (True Project Value)
Local Taxes Deducted to calculate local EAT. Deducted; subject to Double Tax Avoidance Agreements (DTAA) / Foreign Tax Credits.
Withholding Tax (TDS) Ignored (paid upon remittance). Deducted upon actual repatriation (unless credited against parent corporate tax).
Blocked Funds Counted as earned locally. Excluded until reinvested or legally repatriated through transfer pricing/royalties.
Cannibalization / Lost Exports Not visible in subsidiary accounts. Must be deducted as an opportunity cost (lost CFAT from parent exports).
Allocated Overhead Often charged by corporate HQ. Ignored unless incremental cash expenses are actually incurred.

4. Summary Matrix: International Financial Instruments

Instrument Definition Key Exam Distinctions
Eurobond Bonds issued outside the jurisdiction of any single country, denominated in non-local currency. Bearer bonds, gross coupon payments (no withholding tax at source), multinational syndicate.
Foreign Bond Issued by a foreign borrower in a domestic market, denominated in that market's domestic currency. Yankee (USA - $), Samurai (Japan - ¥), Bulldog (UK - £). Subject to domestic SEC/FCA regulations.
FCCB Foreign currency debt carrying an option to convert into equity shares / GDRs at a predetermined strike. Lower coupon rate due to equity upside option. If stock dips, behaves as pure debt; risk of redemption pressure.
ADR vs GDR Negotiable receipts backed by domestic shares deposited with a domestic custodian bank. ADRs: US market only (SEC registered, NYSE/NASDAQ). GDRs: Global reach (London/Luxembourg bourses).
ECB Commercial loans raised from recognized non-resident entities by Indian corporate borrowers. Regulated by RBI under Automatic vs Approval routes; subject to minimum average maturity and all-in-cost ceilings.

5. IFSC & GIFT City: Fast Facts

High-Yield Exam Points on GIFT IFSC:
  • Regulator: International Financial Services Centres Authority (IFSCA) - unified statutory regulator established under IFSCA Act, 2019.
  • Jurisdiction Status: Deemed foreign territory for foreign exchange management and customs purposes; domestic companies inside IFSC enjoy foreign resident status.
  • Corporate Tax Incentive: 100% tax holiday on business income for any 10 consecutive years out of an initial block of 15 years.
  • Minimum Alternate Tax (MAT): Reduced rate of 9% (and nil for units opting for new tax regime).
  • Exemptions: No GST on services provided to IFSC units, no Securities Transaction Tax (STT) or Commodities Transaction Tax (CTT) on IFSC stock exchanges.

6. International Working Capital Techniques

Technique Operational Mechanism Primary Benefit
Multilateral Netting Centralized netting centre computes bilateral intra-group claims across worldwide subsidiaries and offsets balances to a single net payment. Drastically slashes cross-border banking transaction costs, bid-ask spreads, and FX conversion fees.
Centralized Cash Pooling Surplus cash across all operating subsidiaries is swept into a single master account at a global money center. Eliminates idle balances, maximizes interest yields, and minimizes external group borrowing requirements.
Leading & Lagging Accelerating (leading) payments in weak/depreciating currencies; delaying (lagging) payments in strong/appreciating currencies. Reduces currency exchange losses on intra-corporate trade credit without derivative hedging fees.

7. ICAI Exam Pitfalls & Scoring Checklist

⚠️ Pitfall 1: Confusing Spot Rate Quotation Direction in Parity
When using $F_t = S_0 \times \frac{1+r_f}{1+r_d}$, check whether the rate is Direct ($₹/\$$) or Indirect ($\$/₹$). If rate is $₹/\$$, numerator is Indian interest rate and denominator is US interest rate.
⚠️ Pitfall 2: Double Counting Working Capital in NPV
If existing working capital is released immediately upon project start (e.g., TYK PR-4: $15M released from exports), subtract it from initial Year 0 outflow (do not add it as a Year 1 inflow). At terminal year, add back net working capital recovered.
⚠️ Pitfall 3: Withholding Tax Interpretation
In Indian unit questions (e.g., TYK PR-2), note whether Withholding Tax is deducted after corporate tax, or credited against corporate tax. Always provide ICAI's primary method and mention the alternative assumption.
