1. M&A Master Mathematical Formulas

1. Post-Merger Combined Shares & EPS:
$$N_{AB} = N_A + (N_T \times \text{Swap Ratio})$$ $$\text{Combined EPS } (EPS_{AB}) = \frac{\text{PAT}_A + \text{PAT}_T + \text{Synergy}}{N_{AB}}$$
2. Post-Merger Combined Market Capitalization & Price:
$$V_{AB} = V_A + V_T + \text{PV of Synergy} - \text{Cash Consideration}$$ $$\text{Post-Merger MPS } (P_{AB}) = \frac{V_{AB}}{N_{AB}} \quad \text{or} \quad \text{EPS}_{AB} \times (P/E)_{AB}$$
3. Shareholder Wealth Gain/Loss Formulas:
$$\text{Gain to Acquirer Shareholder} = (P_{AB} - P_A) \times N_A$$ $$\text{Gain to Target Shareholder} = (P_{AB} \times \text{Swap Ratio} - P_T) \times N_T$$
4. Maximum Exchange Ratio for Acquirer without EPS Dilution:
$$\text{Max Swap Ratio (EPS)} = \frac{\text{EPS}_T}{\text{EPS}_A}$$
5. Maximum Acquisition Bid Consideration:
$$\text{Max Purchase Consideration} = \text{Target Standalone Market Value} + \text{PV of Synergies} + \text{Cost Savings}$$ $$\text{Max Price per Target Share} = \frac{\text{Max Purchase Consideration}}{N_T}$$

2. Swap Ratio Cheat Sheet Across Methodologies

Valuation Basis Swap Ratio Formula Strategic Implication
Market Price (MPS) Basis $$\text{Swap Ratio} = \frac{\text{MPS}_T}{\text{MPS}_A}$$ Standard market-negotiated ratio. If acquirer P/E is higher, EPS increases automatically (EPS bootstrapping).
Earnings (EPS) Basis $$\text{Swap Ratio} = \frac{\text{EPS}_T}{\text{EPS}_A}$$ Guarantees zero post-merger EPS dilution for both acquirer and target shareholders.
Book Value (BVPS) Basis $$\text{Swap Ratio} = \frac{\text{BVPS}_T}{\text{BVPS}_A}$$ Favored in regulatory/bank mergers (e.g. RBI bank amalgamation schemes). Reflects historical capital base.
Composite / Fair Ratio $$\frac{w_1 \text{SR}_{\text{MPS}} + w_2 \text{SR}_{\text{EPS}} + w_3 \text{SR}_{\text{NAV}}}{w_1 + w_2 + w_3}$$ Dual/Triple method recommended by Court-sanctioned schemes of arrangement to protect minority shareholders.

3. Hostile Takeover Defensive Weapons

Tactical Weapon Timing Operational Mechanism
Poison Pill Pre-Bid Target shareholders receive rights to purchase new shares at deep discounts once raider crosses threshold, massively diluting hostile bidder.
Shark Repellents Pre-Bid Staggered boards (re-electing only 1/3 directors annually) or supermajority voting clauses (80% approval required for mergers).
Golden Parachutes Pre-Bid Extravagant contractual termination severance paid to executives if fired, increasing acquirer cash drain.
White Knight Post-Bid Target invites a friendly third party to launch a higher counter-bid to defeat the hostile raider.
White Squire Post-Bid Friendly investor buys a large strategic block of voting shares pledged to vote with incumbent management.
Pac-Man Defense Post-Bid Target company turns around and launches a hostile tender offer to acquire the original predator firm itself!
Crown Jewel Defense Post-Bid Target sells its most valuable, strategic operating division to make the rest of the company unattractive to the raider.
Greenmail Post-Bid Target repurchases the hostile raider's stock at a hefty premium over market price in exchange for a standstill pledge.

Technique Cash to Parent? Shareholder Distribution Corporate Separation
Sell-off Yes (Cash proceeds) None (Retained by parent) Division absorbed by third party buyer.

5. Maximum Bid Price & Synergies Master Formulas

Core Concept: Determining the absolute upper price ceiling an acquirer can pay without destroying shareholder value.

Metric / Valuation Parameter Formula & Arithmetic Representation Strategic Interpretation
Maximum Cash Price ($P_{\max}$) $P_{\max} = V_T + \Delta V$ Standalone Target value ($V_T$) + Present Value of all Synergies ($\Delta V$).
Maximum Share Exchange Ratio ($ER_{\max}$) $ER_{\max} = \frac{V_T + \Delta V}{V_A} \times \frac{N_A}{N_T}$ At $ER_{\max}$, all synergies transfer to Target shareholders; Acquirer gains 0.
Minimum Share Exchange Ratio ($ER_{\min}$) $ER_{\min} = \frac{P_T}{P_A} = \frac{V_T}{V_A} \times \frac{N_A}{N_T}$ Target shareholders receive standalone value; Acquirer retains 100% of synergies.
Combined Firm Value ($V_{AB}$) $V_{AB} = V_A + V_T + \Delta V - \text{Cash Outflow}$ Total post-merger economic value of the consolidated entity.
Acquirer Gain ($\Delta W_A$) $\Delta W_A = \left[V_{AB} \times \frac{N_A}{N_A + N_{\text{new}}}\right] - V_A$ Value creation accruing to Acquirer's existing shareholders.
Target Gain ($\Delta W_T$) $\Delta W_T = \left[V_{AB} \times \frac{N_{\text{new}}}{N_A + N_{\text{new}}}\right] - V_T$ Total takeover premium and synergy share enjoyed by Target shareholders.

5. ICAI Exam Pitfalls & Traps

⚠️ Pitfall 1: Gross NPA Ratio Direction in Bank Mergers
In bank merger problems (like TYK PR-29), when calculating the swap ratio based on Gross NPA, invert the ratio ($\frac{\text{Strong}}{\text{Weak}}$) because a lower NPA represents superior asset quality! For CAR, MPS, and BVPS, use $\frac{\text{Weak}}{\text{Strong}}$.
⚠️ Pitfall 2: Demerger Shares Entitlement
In Demerger calculations (like TYK PR-22), ensure you calculate the ratio of net worth between the divisions first. The shares issued by the demerged company must match the proportion of net worth transferred.
⚠️ Pitfall 3: Total Post-Merger Earnings
Do not forget to add annual operational synergies to combined earnings when calculating post-merger EPS: $\text{Combined PAT} = \text{PAT}_A + \text{PAT}_T + \text{Synergy}$.
