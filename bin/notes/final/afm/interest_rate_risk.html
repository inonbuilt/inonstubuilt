1. Master Formula Bank

1. Forward Rate Agreement (FRA) Discounted Cash Settlement Formula:
$$\text{Settlement Amount} = \frac{N \times (RR - FR) \times \left(\frac{dtm}{DY}\right)}{1 + RR \times \left(\frac{dtm}{DY}\right)}$$ Where $N$ = Notional, $RR$ = Reference Rate, $FR$ = Forward Rate, $dtm$ = FRA days, $DY$ = Day-count (360 or 365).
2. Bond Price Sensitivity & Duration:
$$D^* = \frac{D_{\text{Macaulay}}}{1 + \frac{y}{m}} \quad \implies \quad \frac{\Delta P}{P} \approx -D^* \times \Delta y + \frac{1}{2} C \times (\Delta y)^2$$
3. Generic Swap Net Settlement:
$$\text{Fixed Leg} = N \times \text{AIC} \times \left(\frac{180}{360}\right)$$ $$\text{Floating Leg} = N \times \text{SOFR} \times \left(\frac{\text{Actual Days}}{360}\right)$$ $$\text{Net Amount Paid by Fixed Payer} = \text{Fixed Leg} - \text{Floating Leg}$$
4. Amortized Cap Option Premium (PVAF Method):
$$\text{Periodic Amortized Premium} = \frac{\text{Total Lump Sum Premium Paid}}{\text{PVAF}(i, t)}$$ Where $i$ = Periodic interest discount rate and $t$ = Number of reset periods.
5. Overnight Index Swap (OIS) Floating Interest:
$$\text{Compounded Amount} = N \times \prod_{k=1}^n \left( 1 + \text{MIBOR}_k \times \frac{d_k}{365} \right) - N$$ Where $d_k$ = days spanned (1 day on weekdays, 2 days on Saturday covering Sunday).

2. FRA Notation & Settlement Guide

Notation ($m \times n$) Fixing Date (Settlement) Underlying Loan Tenor Relevant Reference Rate
$3 \times 6$ FRA 3 months from today $6 - 3 = 3$ months 3-month benchmark rate on fixing date
$6 \times 9$ FRA 6 months from today $9 - 6 = 3$ months 3-month benchmark rate on fixing date
$6 \times 12$ FRA 6 months from today $12 - 6 = 6$ months 6-month benchmark rate on fixing date
Settlement Direction Rule:
• If $RR > FR$: Bank pays Buyer (Borrower hedged successfully against rate hike).
• If $RR < FR$: Buyer pays Bank (Borrower pays cost of insurance when market rates fall).

5. Interest Rate Swaps (IRS) & Overnight Indexed Swaps (OIS)

Core Concept: Contract between two counterparties to exchange interest rate cash flows based on a specified notional principal without exchanging the underlying principal.

Swap Parameter Fixed Rate Payer (Borrower Hedge) Floating Rate Payer (Asset Hedge)
Cash Flow Obligation Pays fixed coupon rate ($R_{\text{swap}}$); receives floating (e.g. MIBOR / SOFR). Pays floating benchmark rate; receives fixed coupon rate.
Market Expectation Anticipates rising interest rates; locks in fixed borrowing cost. Anticipates declining interest rates; benefits from falling floating costs.
Net Settlement Flow $\text{Net Cash Flow} = (R_{\text{floating}} - R_{\text{fixed}}) \times N \times \frac{d}{360\text{ or }365}$ $\text{Net Cash Flow} = (R_{\text{fixed}} - R_{\text{floating}}) \times N \times \frac{d}{360\text{ or }365}$
OIS Structure Floating leg is the geometric compound daily average of overnight rate (e.g. daily overnight MIBOR) settled against fixed rate at maturity.

6. ICAI Exam Pitfalls & Traps

⚠️ Pitfall 1: Forgetting to Discount FRA Settlement
Never just calculate $N \times (RR - FR) \times \frac{dtm}{DY}$. That is the interest at maturity! Settlement happens at fixing date (start of loan period), so you must divide by $[1 + RR \times \frac{dtm}{DY}]$.
⚠️ Pitfall 2: Day-Count Bases in Swaps
Generic fixed swap legs use 30/360 ($\frac{180}{360} = 0.50$). Floating legs use actual days / 360 (e.g., $\frac{181}{360} = 0.502778$). Always watch for the differing denominators!
⚠️ Pitfall 3: Weekend Handling in OIS Calculations
When compiling daily MIBOR tables, Saturday's rate applies for 2 days (Saturday + Sunday) because Sunday is a banking holiday. Multiply by $\frac{2}{365}$.
