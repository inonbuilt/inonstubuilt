1. SA 500: Audit Evidence Gathering Methods (Mnemonic: ICIO-RRR)

Procedure (ICIO-RRR) Audit Action Reliability Hierarchy
1. InspectionExamining records, documents, or tangible physical assets.High for external documents; Medium for internal.
2. ConfirmationDirect written response from third party (debtor, bank).Highest Reliability (Direct external evidence).
3. InquirySeeking information from knowledgeable persons within/outside entity.Inquiry alone is NOT sufficient audit evidence!
4. ObservationLooking at a process or procedure being performed by others.Limited to the point in time observed.
5. RecalculationChecking mathematical accuracy of documents or records.High for mathematical correctness.
6. ReperformanceIndependent execution of procedures or controls by auditor.High for operating effectiveness of controls.
7. Analytical ProceduresEvaluations of financial info through analysis of plausible relationships.High when controls over data are effective.

2. SA 501: Physical Inventory Count Verification

📦 Attendance at Physical Inventory Count is Mandatory

If inventory is material, the auditor MUST attend physical inventory counting unless impracticable.
If Impracticable (e.g., hazardous cargo, remote location): The auditor must perform alternative audit procedures (inspect subsequent sales records, purchase documentation). If not possible to obtain evidence → Modify opinion under SA 705 (NFRA Case Precedent!).

  • Inventory Held by Third Parties (Warehouses): Request confirmation from third party under SA 505, or perform inspection/attend count.

3. SA 505: External Confirmations Protocol

Confirmation Type Mechanism Audit Evidence Value
Positive Confirmation Requesting party to reply directly to auditor indicating whether they agree or disagree, or providing requested information. High Reliability. Non-response requires follow-up or alternative procedures.
Negative Confirmation Requesting party to respond ONLY if they disagree with the information provided. Low Reliability. Permitted only when RMM is low, large number of small balances, and very low exception rate expected.
⚠️ Management Refuses to Allow Confirmation Request

If management refuses to allow the auditor to send a confirmation request:

  1. Inquire as to management's reasons and seek audit evidence as to validity and reasonableness;
  2. Evaluate implications on RMM and risk of fraud;
  3. Perform alternative audit procedures;
  4. If refusal is unreasonable or evidence cannot be obtained → Communicate to TCWG and modify audit opinion under SA 705.

4. SA 510: Initial Audit Engagements & Opening Balances

  • Objective: Determine whether opening balances contain misstatements materially affecting current period financials, and accounting policies are consistently applied.
  • Audit Procedures: Read most recent financial statements and predecessor auditor's report; check whether prior period closing balances correctly brought forward; test opening inventory valuation.
  • Reporting Modifications:
    • If opening balances contain misstatement not corrected → Qualified or Adverse Opinion on current P&L.
    • If inability to obtain evidence regarding opening balances → Qualified or Disclaimer on P&L and Cash Flows, with Unmodified on Balance Sheet (split opinion).

5. SA 530: Audit Sampling Rules

Sampling Concept Statutory Audit Definition & Application
Sampling Risk Risk that auditor's conclusion based on a sample may be different from conclusion reached if entire population were tested.
Risk of Incorrect Acceptance Test of details concludes material misstatement does not exist when it actually does. Affects audit effectiveness → Leads to inappropriate audit opinion!
Risk of Incorrect Rejection Test of details concludes material misstatement exists when it does not. Affects audit efficiency (extra work).
Stratification Dividing a population into sub-populations (strata) having similar characteristics, reducing sample size without increasing risk.

6. SA 550: Related Parties Verification Protocol

  • Heightened Risk: Related parties operate outside normal market forces, creating immense opportunities for fraudulent financial reporting.
  • Auditor Procedures: Review bank and legal confirmations; inspect significant transactions outside normal course of business; evaluate contract terms for commercial rationale.
  • Undisclosed Related Parties Found: Promptly communicate to engagement team; request management to identify all transactions with the newly identified party; perform detailed substantive testing; re-evaluate fraud risk.
