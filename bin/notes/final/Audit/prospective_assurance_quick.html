1. SAE 3400: Examination of Prospective Financial Information (PFI)

PFI is financial information based on assumptions about events that may occur in the future and possible actions by an entity.

Dimension Forecast Projection
Basis of Preparation Based on Best-Estimate Assumptions (events management expects to take place as of date of info). Based on Hypothetical Assumptions (what-if scenarios about future events not necessarily expected to happen) OR a mix of best-estimate + hypothetical.
Typical Use Budgeting, IPO prospectus, bank borrowing. Feasibility studies for new product launch, bidding for a new venture.
Assurance Level Moderate Level of Assurance regarding reasonableness of assumptions, and Negative Assurance on preparation. Auditor NEVER provides assurance on whether actual results will achieve projections!
Mandatory Caveats in SAE 3400 Report

1. Statement that actual results are likely to be different from PFI since anticipated events frequently do not occur as expected, and variation may be material.
2. In case of projection: Explicit statement that the projection was prepared for [specified purpose] and must not be used for any other purpose.

2. SAE 3402: Assurance Reports on Controls at a Service Organisation

Applies to service auditors reporting on internal controls at third-party service organisations (e.g., payroll processing, cloud data center, registrar & transfer agents).

Report Category Scope / What It Covers Period Covered
Type 1 Report • Description of service org's system.
• Design and implementation of controls as of a specified date.
• No testing of operating effectiveness!		 As of a specific point in time (e.g., as at March 31).
Type 2 Report • Description of service org's system.
• Design and implementation of controls.
• Operating effectiveness of controls over a specified period (tests of controls performed).		 Covers a period of time (typically minimum 6 months).
User Auditor's Reliance (Link to SA 402)

A user auditor can use a Type 2 Report as audit evidence to reduce control risk assessment. A Type 1 Report only assists in understanding controls but provides ZERO evidence of operating effectiveness throughout the audit period.

3. SAE 3420: Assurance on Compilation of Pro Forma Financial Info

Purpose of Pro Forma Information

Shows the unadjusted financial information of an entity as if a proposed transaction (e.g., merger, demerger, acquisition) had occurred at an earlier date chosen for illustration purposes. Commonly included in an Offer Document / Prospectus.

Practitioner's Objective Mandatory Reporting Expression
Reasonable Assurance Obtain reasonable assurance about whether the pro forma financial information has been compiled, in all material respects, by responsible party on the basis of applicable criteria.
Opinion Statement "In our opinion, the pro forma financial information has been properly compiled on the basis stated, and such basis is consistent with the accounting policies of the entity."
Important Clarification The practitioner has no responsibility to update or re-issue any reports on underlying financial information used to compile pro forma information.

4. Summary of Assurance Levels Across Standards

Standard Area Nature of Assurance
SA 200–720 Historical Financial Statements Reasonable Assurance (High)
SRE 2400 / 2410 Review of Historical / Interim FS Limited Assurance (Moderate)
SAE 3400 Prospective Financial Information (Forecast/Projection) Moderate Assurance on assumptions + Negative on preparation
SAE 3402 Type 1 Service Org Controls Design Reasonable Assurance on design at a point in time
SAE 3402 Type 2 Service Org Controls Operating Effectiveness Reasonable Assurance on operating effectiveness over period
SAE 3420 Pro Forma Financial Information Reasonable Assurance on proper compilation
SRS 4400 / 4410 Agreed-Upon Procedures / Compilation No Assurance
