1. IRAC Norms & NPA Classification Criteria

An asset becomes a Non-Performing Asset (NPA) when it ceases to generate income for the bank.

Facility Type NPA Trigger Criteria
Term Loans Interest and/or installment of principal remains overdue for > 90 days.
Cash Credit / Overdraft (CC/OD) Account remains "Out of Order" for > 90 days.
Bills Purchased & Discounted Bill remains overdue for a period of > 90 days.
Agricultural Advances • Short duration crop loans: Overdue for 2 crop seasons.
• Long duration crop loans (crops > 1 yr): Overdue for 1 crop season.
Credit Card Accounts Minimum Amount Due not paid fully within 90 days from next statement date.
Definition of "Out of Order" in CC / OD

An account is treated as "out of order" if:
1. Outstanding Balance exceeds Sanctioned Limit / Drawing Power continuously for 90 days; OR
2. Balance is within limit, but there are no credits continuously for 90 days, or credits are insufficient to cover interest debited during the same period.

Borrower-Wise Classification Principle

NPA classification is determined borrower-wise, NOT facility-wise! If one facility of a borrower turns NPA, all facilities granted to that borrower must be classified as NPA. Exception: Bills co-accepted by other banks.

3. Audit Verification of Advances & Drawing Power (DP)

Drawing Power (DP) Calculation Rules

• Paid Stocks: DP is calculated strictly on paid-up inventory (Gross Stock minus Creditors / Trade Payables).
• Stock Audit: Mandatory for large exposure working capital accounts (typically limits ≥ ₹5 Crore).
• Old Stock Statements: If stock statements are older than 3 months, the account is deemed irregular, and DP cannot be allowed → potential NPA trigger!

4. Long Form Audit Report (LFAR) & Regulatory Filings

Report / Filing Coverage & Filing Requirements
Long Form Audit Report (LFAR) Detailed questionnaire covering advances, cash, deposits, internal controls, fraud, KYC/AML compliance. Branch LFAR submitted to Statutory Central Auditors (SCAs) within prescribed timelines.
Statutory Liquidity Ratio (SLR) & CRR Audit of 12 selected odd dates to verify compliance with Sec 24 (SLR) and Sec 42 of RBI Act (CRR). Weekly reporting accuracy verified.
Basel III CRAR (Capital to Risk-Weighted Assets) Minimum Tier 1 Capital: 7%; Minimum Total Capital (CRAR): 9% (plus Capital Conservation Buffer 2.50%).
