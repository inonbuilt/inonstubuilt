Chapter 10: Review of Financial Information (SRE 2400 & SRE 2410)

1. SRE 2400 (Revised) vs SRE 2410 Master Distinction

Distinguishing Factor SRE 2400 (Revised) SRE 2410
Subject Matter Historical financial statements of an entity. Interim financial information (quarterly / half-yearly).
Who Performs? Practitioner who is not necessarily the statutory auditor. Independent Auditor of the entity who also audits the annual FS.
Assurance Level Limited Assurance (Negative form of conclusion). Limited Assurance (Negative form of conclusion).
Understanding of Controls Understanding entity & environment sufficient to identify areas where material misstatements are likely to arise. Auditor already possesses updated understanding of internal controls from the annual audit process.

2. Review Procedures & Depth of Examination

Core Two Pillars: Inquiry + Analytical Procedures

A review engagement consists primarily of making inquiries of management and analytical procedures. It does NOT require testing internal controls, physical verification of assets, or obtaining third-party confirmations (unless reason to believe info is false).

Procedure Category Specific Review Actions
Inquiries • Inquire regarding accounting policies and consistency.
• Inquire regarding major accounting estimates, provisions, and contingencies.
• Inquire regarding significant journal entries and adjustments around period-end.
• Inquire regarding events subsequent to review period.
Analytical Procedures • Compare current period results with prior periods and budgets.
• Ratio analysis (gross margin, inventory turnover, receivable days).
• Investigate unusual fluctuations or inconsistencies.
Written Representations Mandatory written representations from management acknowledging responsibility for FS, internal controls, and completeness of info.

3. Review Conclusions Matrix

The practitioner forms a conclusion expressed in negative form:

Situation Type of Conclusion Wording Formulation
Unmodified Conclusion Clean Conclusion "Nothing has come to our attention that causes us to believe that the accompanying financial statements do not give a true and fair view..."
Material Departure from AFRF Qualified Conclusion "Except for the effects of matter described... nothing has come to our attention..."
Pervasive Departure from AFRF Adverse Conclusion "The accompanying financial statements do not give a true and fair view in accordance with AFRF."
Pervasive Scope Limitation Disclaimer of Conclusion "We do not express a conclusion on the accompanying financial statements."

4. SEBI LODR Regulation 33 Fast-Track Rules

Quarterly Financial Reporting Timelines

• Quarterly Submission: Listed entities must submit quarterly standalone & consolidated financial results to stock exchanges within 45 days of the end of the quarter (along with Limited Review Report or Audit Report).
• Last Quarter Option: For the last quarter, submit audited annual results within 60 days of the end of the financial year.
• Statutory Requirement: Limited Review of listed entities must be conducted in accordance with SRE 2410 by an auditor holding a valid Peer Review certificate.
