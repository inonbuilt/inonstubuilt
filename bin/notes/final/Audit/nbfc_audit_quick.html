Chapter 14 Unit 2: NBFC Audit (Scale-Based Regulation SBR)

⚡ Fast-Track High-Yield Revision ICAI New Scheme 2026–2027

1. Registration Criteria & The 50-50 Principal Business Test

Under Section 45-IA of RBI Act 1934, every NBFC must obtain a Certificate of Registration (CoR) and maintain minimum Net Owned Funds (NOF) (glide path up to ₹10 Crore).

The 50-50 Principal Business Test

A company is treated as an NBFC if it satisfies BOTH conditions:
1. Financial Assets Test: Financial assets constitute more than 50% of total assets.
2. Financial Income Test: Income from financial assets constitutes more than 50% of total gross income.

2. RBI Scale-Based Regulation (SBR) Framework

RBI regulates NBFCs based on systemic significance through a 4-tiered structure:

SBR Layer Categories of NBFCs Included Key Regulatory Tightness
1. Base Layer (NBFC-BL) • Non-deposit taking NBFCs with asset size < ₹ 1,000 Crore.
• NBFC-P2P (Peer-to-Peer), Account Aggregators (AA), NOFHC.		 Basic prudential norms; NOF target ₹10 Cr; simplified governance.
2. Middle Layer (NBFC-ML) • ALL Deposit-taking NBFCs (NBFC-D) regardless of asset size.
• Non-deposit NBFCs with asset size ≥ ₹ 1,000 Crore.
• Primary Dealers, Core Investment Companies (CICs), HFCs, Infrastructure Debt Funds (IDF).		 Stricter capital adequacy, board committees mandatory (Audit, NRC, Risk Mgmt).
3. Upper Layer (NBFC-UL) • Top 10 NBFCs identified by RBI based on quantitative & qualitative scoring methodology.
• Always includes top NBFCs in asset size.		 Bank-like regulatory intensity, mandatory listing within 3 years, Common Equity Tier 1 (CET1) 9%.
4. Top Layer (NBFC-TL) • Empty by default.
• Activated by RBI if an NBFC-UL exhibits extreme systemic risk spillover.		 Higher capital add-ons, intense regulatory surveillance.

3. Asset Classification & Provisioning Norms

Harmonisation of NPA Norms with Banks

NPA recognition for NBFCs across all layers is now harmonized to > 90 days overdue (replacing the old 180-day norm).

Asset Class Overdue Period Standard Provision
Standard Assets Regular servicing / ≤ 90 days 0.40% of total outstanding
Sub-Standard NPA for ≤ 12 months 10% (Ind AS: ECL based)
Doubtful NPA for > 12 months • Up to 1 yr (D1): 20% secured, 100% unsecured
• 1 to 3 yrs (D2): 30% secured, 100% unsecured
• > 3 yrs (D3): 50% secured, 100% unsecured
Loss Assets Identified as uncollectible 100% write-off or provision

4. NBFC Auditor's Report & Exception Reporting to RBI

Mandatory Exception Reporting to RBI within 7 Days

Under the NBFC Auditor's Report (Reserve Bank) Directions, 2016, if the auditor observes that the NBFC:
1. Has not obtained CoR or is not entitled to hold CoR;
2. Fails to meet NOF criteria;
3. Has failed to comply with Prudential Norms on capital adequacy, asset classification, or provisioning;
The auditor has a statutory duty to send an exception report directly to the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) within 7 days of noticing the violation, irrespective of whether management agrees.
