1. SA 240: Fraud Triangle & Section 143(12) Reporting

Fraud is an intentional act involving deception to obtain an unjust or illegal advantage. Two types of misstatements relevant to the auditor: Fraudulent Financial Reporting and Misappropriation of Assets.

🔺 The Fraud Triangle: O-P-R Mnemonic
  • Opportunity: Absence of controls, ineffective governance, complex organizational structure, override of controls.
  • Pressure / Incentive: Financial distress, aggressive earnings targets, bonuses tied to revenue metrics.
  • Rationalization / Attitude: Justification of dishonest acts ("everyone does it", "temporary borrowing").

Statutory Fraud Reporting: Section 143(12) vs. Rule 13

Fraud Amount Reporting Authority Statutory Procedure & Timeline
≥ ₹1 Crore (Individual Fraud) Central Government (MCA) (Sec 143(12)) 1. Report to Board / Audit Committee within 2 days seeking reply within 45 days.
2. On receipt of reply (or expiry of 45 days), forward report in Form ADT-4 to Central Government within 15 days.
< ₹1 Crore Audit Committee / Board of Directors Report within 2 days specifying nature of fraud, approximate amount, and parties involved. Company discloses in Board's Report.

2. SA 250: Direct Effect vs. Indirect Operating Laws

Law Category Scope & Examples Auditor Responsibility
Category 1:
Direct Effect		 Directly determine reported amounts and disclosures in financial statements (e.g., Tax laws, Companies Act, Pension laws). Obtain sufficient appropriate audit evidence regarding compliance.
Category 2:
Indirect / Operating		 Fundamental to operating activities, operating license, or solvency (e.g., Environmental laws, Factory safety, Labour laws). Limited to specified audit procedures: inquiring of management and inspecting correspondence with regulatory licensing bodies.

3. SA 260 & SA 265: TCWG Communication & Deficiencies

📢 Significant Deficiencies under SA 265

A Significant Deficiency is a deficiency or combination of deficiencies in internal control that, in the auditor's professional judgment, is of sufficient importance to merit the attention of Those Charged with Governance (TCWG).
Mandatory Written Form: The auditor must communicate significant deficiencies in internal control in writing to TCWG on a timely basis (Management Letter / Letter of Weakness).

  • Matters to Communicate under SA 260: Auditor's responsibilities, planned scope and timing of audit, significant findings (accounting policies, accounting estimates, difficulties encountered), auditor independence (for listed entities).

4. SA 299: Joint Audit Liability Matrix

Individual Liability (Divided Work) Joint & Several Liability (Shared Duties)
• Audit work allocated to the specific joint auditor in the agreed work allocation document.
• Propriety of audit procedures applied to assigned work.
• Verification of records, assets, or branch offices specifically allocated to him. 		• Work which is not divided and performed jointly.
• Decisions taken by all joint auditors regarding common audit areas.
• Financial statements disclosure compliance with statutory format.
• Ensuring the audit report complies with applicable statutes.
⚠️ Dissenting Opinion Rule in Joint Audits

Joint auditors are NOT bound to present a unanimous opinion. If joint auditors disagree, each dissenting auditor is entitled to issue a separate audit report. The majority opinion does not bind the minority!

5. SA 402: Service Organizations (Type 1 vs. Type 2 Reports)

Report Type Description & Scope Auditor Reliance Value
Type 1 Report Report on the description and design of controls at the service organization as at a specified date. Provides understanding of controls; CANNOT be used as basis for reducing control risk (no operating testing).
Type 2 Report Report on description, design, and operating effectiveness of controls throughout a specified period. Provides audit evidence on operating effectiveness; user auditor can rely to reduce substantive testing.
