1. SA 700 / 705 / 706 Opinion Formulation Matrix

The auditor forms an opinion on whether the financial statements are prepared, in all material respects, in accordance with the applicable financial reporting framework (AFRF).

Auditor's Assessment Material but NOT Pervasive Material AND Pervasive
Financial Statements are Materially Misstated (Disagreement / GAAP Departure) Qualified Opinion ("Except for...") Adverse Opinion ("Do not present fairly...")
Inability to Obtain SAAE (Scope Limitation / Records Destroyed) Qualified Opinion ("Except for possible effects...") Disclaimer of Opinion ("Do not express an opinion")
Emphasis of Matter (EOM) vs Other Matter (OM) Paragraph (SA 706)

• EOM Paragraph (SA 706): Refers to a matter appropriately presented or disclosed in the FS that is fundamental to users' understanding (e.g., exceptional litigation, early adoption of new AS, major catastrophe). Never a substitute for modified opinion or KAM!
• OM Paragraph (SA 706): Refers to a matter other than those presented or disclosed in FS relevant to users' understanding of audit, auditor's responsibilities, or report (e.g., prior period audited by predecessor auditor).

2. SA 701 Key Audit Matters (KAM) Decision Logic

Applicability: Mandatory for audits of complete sets of general purpose financial statements of listed entities and circumstances when auditor decides or law requires.

Funnel Step Scope / Description
Step 1: Broad Universe Matters communicated with Those Charged with Governance (TCWG) under SA 260.
Step 2: Auditor Attention Matters that required significant auditor attention in performing the audit (high risk areas, high management judgment/estimates, significant unusual transactions).
Step 3: KAM Selection Matters of most significance in the audit of the financial statements of the current period.
Crucial SA 701 Examiner Traps

• KAM is NOT: (1) A substitute for disclosures in FS; (2) A substitute for a modified opinion under SA 705; (3) A substitute for Going Concern reporting under SA 570; (4) A separate opinion on individual matters.
• Disclaimer of Opinion: Communicating KAM is strictly prohibited when the auditor disclaims an opinion under SA 705 (unless required by law).

3. CARO 2020 Applicability & Exemption Test

Applies to every company including a foreign company, EXCEPT the following categories:

Exempt Category Statutory Criteria / Limits
1. Banking & Insurance Banking companies (Banking Regulation Act, 1949) & Insurance companies (Insurance Act, 1938).
2. Section 8 Company Companies licensed under Section 8 of Companies Act, 2013 (charitable objectives).
3. One Person Co (OPC) & Small Co OPC under Sec 2(62) and Small Company under Sec 2(85) (Paid-up Capital ≤ ₹4 Cr and Turnover ≤ ₹40 Cr).
4. Private Limited Company Exemption A private company (not subsidiary/holding of public co) satisfying ALL THREE tests on balance sheet date:
(a) Paid-up Capital + Reserves: ≤ ₹1 Crore.
(b) Total Borrowings (Banks/FIs): ≤ ₹1 Crore at any point of time during FY.
(c) Total Revenue (including discontinued): ≤ ₹10 Crore during FY as per Schedule III.
Consolidated FS CARO 2020 does not apply to CFS except Clause (xxi) (qualifications in subsidiary/associate/JV CARO reports).

5. Section 143 Inquiries, Reporting & Fraud (Sec 143(12))

Section 143(1) Propriety Inquiries (Mnemonic: L-D-B-P-P-S)

Auditor inquires into 6 specific items: (1) Loans on security properly secured & terms not prejudicial; (2) Transactions represented merely by Book entries prejudicial; (3) Sale of shares/securities at price below purchase price (other than banking/investment co); (4) Loans & advances shown as Deposits; (5) Personal expenses charged to revenue account; (6) Shares allotted for cash & cash received entered in books.
Reporting trigger: Only if auditor finds adverse findings! If satisfied, no mention needed.

Fraud Amount Threshold Statutory Reporting Channel & Timelines (Sec 143(12) + Rule 13)
₹ 1 Crore or More
(Against the company by officers/employees)		 • Report to Audit Committee / Board within 2 days seeking reply in 45 days.
• On receipt of reply (or expiry of 45 days), report to Central Government (MCA) within 15 days in Form ADT-4.
Less than ₹ 1 Crore • Report to Audit Committee / Board within 2 days.
• Disclose in Board's Report (nature of fraud, amount, parties involved if remedial action taken). No Form ADT-4 to Central Govt.
