CGST ACT, 2017 • SECTIONS 22 TO 30 • RULES 8 TO 26

Chapter 8: Registration under GST

Exhaustive textbook revision notes on the entry gate of GST compliance. Covers State-wise registration architecture, PAN-based threshold limits (₹10L/₹20L/₹40L), Section 24 mandatory registration without threshold, Form GST REG-01 filing, Aadhaar and biometric verification, CTP/NRTP advance deposits, and cancellation/revocation timelines.

8.1 Architecture of Registration under GST

Registration legally recognizes a person as a supplier under GST, authorizes tax collection, and unlocks Input Tax Credit (ITC). Key pillars of the GST registration regime:

📌 Architectural Principles:
• State-Wise Registration: GST does NOT have centralized registration. A supplier must register in every State/UT from where he makes a taxable supply.
• No Tax-Specific Registration: A single registration covers CGST, SGST/UTGST, IGST and Compensation Cess.
• PAN-Linked: GSTIN is a 15-digit alphanumeric code built on the entity's Permanent Account Number (PAN).
• Multiple Places of Business: A person with multiple places of business in a State may obtain a single registration (with 1 PPoB and multiple APoBs) OR separate registrations for each place of business [Section 25(2)].
• Distinct Persons [Section 25(4) & 25(5)]: Separate registrations under the same PAN (whether in different States or within the same State) are treated as distinct persons.

Section 22 Persons Liable for Registration — The 3-Tier Threshold Structure

Every supplier is liable to be registered in the State or Union territory from where he makes a taxable supply of goods or services or both, if his Aggregate Turnover in a financial year exceeds the prescribed threshold limit:

Threshold Limit Category of States / Territory Scope of Supply
₹10 Lakhs Special Category States (M-M-N-T):
Manipur, Mizoram, Nagaland, Tripura		 Suppliers of Goods OR Services OR Both (Applicable uniformly).
₹20 Lakhs Specified States (P-U-M-A-T-S):
Puducherry, Uttarakhand, Meghalaya, Arunachal Pradesh, Telangana, Sikkim 		Suppliers of Goods OR Services OR Both.
₹20 Lakhs All Other States / UTs Suppliers engaged in supply of Services OR Mixed Supplies (Goods + Services).
₹40 Lakhs
Notfn 10/2019-CT		 All Other States / UTs (e.g. Delhi, Maharashtra, Gujarat, UP, Tamil Nadu, etc.) Suppliers engaged EXCLUSIVELY in intra-State supply of GOODS.

🚫 Persons NOT Eligible for the Higher ₹40 Lakh Threshold:

The higher threshold of ₹40 Lakhs is DISALLOWED (and threshold drops to ₹20 Lakhs) for:
1. Persons making any inter-State outward supplies (requires compulsory registration u/s 24);
2. Persons required to register compulsorily under Section 24;
3. Persons engaged in the manufacture of notified goods:
  • Ice cream and other edible ice;
  • Pan masala;
  • Tobacco and manufactured tobacco substitutes;
  • Aerated water;
  • Fly ash bricks, building bricks, brick earth, roofing tiles;
4. Persons supplying goods through an E-Commerce Operator required to collect TCS;
5. Persons registered voluntarily under Section 25(3).

Section 2(6) Computation of "Aggregate Turnover" (All-India PAN Basis)

Aggregate turnover is computed on an all-India basis for the same PAN:

🧮 Aggregate Turnover Equation:

INCLUSIONS (+) :
• Taxable supplies of goods and services (intra-State & inter-State);
• Exempt supplies (including nil rated, wholly exempt, and non-taxable supplies like petrol/alcohol);
• Exports of goods or services or both;
• Inter-State supplies to distinct persons under the same PAN;
• Supplies made on behalf of principals by an agent!

EXCLUSIONS (−) :
• Central tax (CGST), State tax (SGST), UT tax (UTGST), Integrated tax (IGST), and Compensation Cess;
• Value of inward supplies on which tax is payable under Reverse Charge Mechanism (RCM)!

Section 23 Persons Not Liable for Registration

Under Section 23 of the CGST Act, the following persons are NOT required to obtain registration:

  • Exclusively Non-Taxable / Exempt Supplies [Sec 23(1)(a)]: Any person engaged exclusively in the business of supplying goods or services that are not liable to tax (e.g. alcoholic liquor, petrol, diesel) or wholly exempt from tax under Section 11 / Section 6.
  • Agriculturists [Sec 23(1)(b)]: An agriculturist, to the extent of supply of produce out of cultivation of land (carried on by self, family, or hired labour under personal supervision).
  • Suppliers under 100% RCM [Notfn 5/2017-CT]: Persons who are only engaged in making supplies of taxable goods or services, the total tax on which is liable to be paid by the recipient under Reverse Charge Mechanism.
  • Inter-State Service Providers up to ₹20L/₹10L [Notfn 10/2017-IT]: Exemption from compulsory registration under Section 24(i) for service providers making inter-State supplies, provided aggregate turnover ≤ ₹20L (₹10L in MMNT).
  • Suppliers of Goods via ECO [Notfn 34/2023-CT]: Persons supplying goods through an ECO exempt from compulsory registration u/s 24(ix) subject to: turnover ≤ threshold, intra-State supply only, obtaining enrolment number on portal, and having a valid PAN.

Section 24 Compulsory Registration in Specified Cases (Zero Threshold)

Notwithstanding anything in Section 22(1), the following categories of persons MUST COMPULSORILY REGISTER, even if their aggregate turnover is ₹1:

1. Inter-State Taxable Outward Supply:
Suppliers of goods making inter-State outward taxable supplies (Exception: Service providers ≤ ₹20L/₹10L & handicraft dealers).
2. Casual Taxable Persons (CTP):
CTPs making taxable supply in a State where they have no fixed place of business.
3. Reverse Charge Recipients:
Persons required to pay tax under Reverse Charge Mechanism under Section 9(3) / 9(4).
4. Non-Resident Taxable Persons:
NRTPs making taxable supplies in India.
5. E-Commerce Operators (Sec 9(5)):
ECOs liable to pay tax on passenger transport, accommodation, housekeeping, restaurant services.
6. TDS Deductors (Sec 51):
Government departments/authorities required to deduct tax at source.
7. Agents of Taxable Persons:
Persons who make taxable supply of goods/services on behalf of other taxable persons.
8. Input Service Distributor (ISD):
Mandatory registration for distributing input service credit to distinct persons.
9. TCS E-Commerce Operators:
ECOs required to collect tax at source under Section 52.
10. Cross-Border OIDAR & Gaming:
Suppliers of OIDAR or online money gaming from outside India to non-taxable recipients in India.

Section 25 Registration Procedure & Verification Timelines

📝 Form GST REG-01 (Two-Part Submission)

• Part A: Declare PAN, Mobile No., Email ID & State/UT. Online validation generates a Temporary Reference Number (TRN).
• Part B: Submit detailed application using TRN within 15 days, incorporating business details, PPoB, bank account, and authorised signatory.
• Timeline: Apply within 30 days of becoming liable (or 5 days prior for CTP/NRTP).

⏱️ Effective Date of Registration

• Applied within 30 days: Effective date is the date on which the person becomes liable to registration! (Can issue revised tax invoices u/s 31(3) for past supplies).
• Applied AFTER 30 days: Effective date is the date of grant of registration! (Cannot issue revised invoices for intervening period).

🔐 Aadhaar Authentication & Biometric Verification [Rule 8(4A) & Rule 9]

Category of Applicant Verification Route Approval Timeline
Opted & Successful Aadhaar Authentication Deemed authenticated; no site physical verification required normally. Grant within 7 working days from application date.
Failed / Not Opted for Aadhaar OR High-Risk Profile Mandatory physical verification of business premises in presence of applicant. Notice in REG-03 within 30 days. Within 30 days from submission of application.

Sections 28, 29 & 30 Amendment, Cancellation & Revocation of Registration

✏️ Amendment of Registration [Sec 28 & Rule 19]

• Core Fields: Legal name of business, PPoB/APoB, addition/deletion of partners, directors, Karta. Requires proper officer approval within 15 working days!
• Non-Core Fields: Mobile, email, minor changes → System auto-approves instantly.
• Change in PAN: Requires fresh registration; cannot be amended!

❌ Cancellation & Revocation [Sec 29 & 30]

• Grounds for Cancellation: Non-filing of returns (Composition: 3 months beyond due date; Regular: Continuous 6 months / 2 quarters), registration obtained by fraud.
• Revocation Timeline [Sec 30]: Apply in Form GST REG-21 within 90 days from date of service of order (extendable by Commissioner up to further 180 days).

TYK Master Cases ICAI Test Your Knowledge — Solved Practical Problems (1 to 6)

🎯 TYK Question 1 • Mahadev Enterprises (Luxury Watches & Inter-State Branch)

Facts: Mahadev Enterprises, a sole proprietorship in Delhi, trades in luxury watches (goods). Aggregate turnover in current FY is ₹32 Lakhs (entirely intra-State in Delhi). In September, it opens a branch in Assam and supplies watches worth ₹5 Lakhs from Assam. Determine registration liability in Delhi and Assam.

Statutory Analysis & Solution:

  • Assam is a regular State (not one of the 4 Special Category States with ₹10L limit: Manipur, Mizoram, Nagaland, Tripura). Assam has a ₹40 Lakh threshold for exclusive goods dealers.
  • Total all-India aggregate turnover = ₹32 Lakhs (Delhi) + ₹5 Lakhs (Assam) = ₹37 Lakhs.
  • Since turnover does not exceed ₹40 Lakhs and supplies are exclusively intra-State goods in both locations, Mahadev Enterprises is NOT LIABLE to register in either Delhi or Assam.
🎯 TYK Question 2 • LMN Pvt. Ltd. (Exclusive Goods Dealer in Tamil Nadu)

Facts: LMN Pvt. Ltd., Coimbatore (Tamil Nadu), exclusively manufactures intra-State taxable garments. Total turnover is ₹36 Lakhs. It now plans to make an inter-State outward supply of garments worth ₹25,000 to a customer in Kerala. Advise LMN Pvt. Ltd.

Solution:
• As long as LMN Pvt. Ltd. makes ONLY intra-State supplies of goods, it enjoys the higher threshold of ₹40 Lakhs under Notification 10/2019-CT.
• The moment it makes an inter-State outward supply of goods, condition (a) of Notification 10/2019 is breached, AND Section 24(i) triggers mandatory registration with ZERO threshold!
• Conclusion: LMN Pvt. Ltd. must obtain registration BEFORE making the inter-State supply of ₹25,000!

🎯 TYK Question 3 • SNP Pvt. Ltd. (Manufacturer of Notified Goods)

Facts: SNP Pvt. Ltd. in Coimbatore manufactures pan masala and ice cream. Turnover during current FY is ₹22 Lakhs (all intra-State in Tamil Nadu). Examine whether SNP Pvt. Ltd. is liable to register.

Solution:
• Under Notification No. 10/2019-CT, manufacturers of pan masala, ice cream, tobacco, aerated water, and building bricks are expressly excluded from the enhanced ₹40 Lakhs threshold.
• Consequently, the threshold limit reverts to ₹20 Lakhs under Section 22(1).
• Since turnover of ₹22 Lakhs exceeds ₹20 Lakhs, SNP Pvt. Ltd. is COMPULSORILY LIABLE TO REGISTER under Section 22!

🎯 TYK Question 4 • Rishabh Enterprises (Goods + Minor Service Revenue)

Facts: Rishabh Enterprises in Jammu & Kashmir sells electrical hardware (goods) with turnover of ₹35 Lakhs, and also earns commission of ₹1,50,000 for providing agency services. Advise on registration liability.

Solution:
• Enhanced threshold of ₹40 Lakhs applies ONLY to persons engaged exclusively in the supply of goods.
• Since Rishabh Enterprises supplies services (commission ₹1.5L), it becomes a mixed supplier. The threshold limit drops to ₹20 Lakhs!
• Total aggregate turnover = ₹35,00,000 + ₹1,50,000 = ₹36,50,000 (exceeds ₹20L).
• Conclusion: Rishabh Enterprises is LIABLE to be registered.

🎯 TYK Question 5 • Multi-State Aggregate Turnover Computation

Facts: An entity operates under same PAN across 3 States:
• Unit 1 (Maharashtra): Taxable supply = ₹14L; Exempt supply = ₹3L; Inward supplies under RCM = ₹4L.
• Unit 2 (Tripura - Special Category): Taxable supply = ₹4L; Non-taxable petrol = ₹2L.
Determine whether entity is liable to register in Maharashtra and Tripura.

Computation & Solution:
• Aggregate Turnover under Section 2(6):
  Maharashtra: ₹14L (Taxable) + ₹3L (Exempt) = ₹17L. (Inward RCM ₹4L is excluded!).
  Tripura: ₹4L (Taxable) + ₹2L (Non-taxable petrol included in exempt) = ₹6L.
  Total All-India Aggregate Turnover = ₹17L + ₹6L = ₹23 Lakhs!
• Threshold Applicable: Because the entity makes taxable supplies from Tripura (a Special Category State), the threshold limit across India is reduced to ₹10 Lakhs!
• Conclusion: Since ₹23 Lakhs > ₹10 Lakhs, the entity is LIABLE TO REGISTER IN BOTH Maharashtra AND Tripura!

🎯 TYK Question 6 • Rajesh Dynamics (Comprehensive Registration Scenarios)

Facts: Rajesh Dynamics (Chennai HO) operates several businesses: (1) Branch in Telangana (turnover ₹18L goods); (2) Supplies agricultural produce directly grown on land; (3) Acts as commission agent issuing invoice in its own name for principal (turnover ₹8L). Determine registration requirements.

Solution:
• Agent under Section 24(vii): A person supplying goods on behalf of other taxable persons where invoices are issued in agent's own name is compulsorily required to register under Section 24(vii) with zero threshold!
• Agricultural produce: Excluded under Section 23(1)(b) only if grown directly by cultivation of land.
• Conclusion: Rajesh Dynamics is compulsorily required to register under Section 24(vii) on account of acting as an agent.
