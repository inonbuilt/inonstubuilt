CGST ACT, 2017 • SECTIONS 16 TO 21 • RULES 36 TO 45

Chapter 7: Input Tax Credit (ITC)

The core engine and lifeline of the GST regime. Complete statutory master notes covering Section 16 eligibility criteria, the 180-day supplier payment rule, Section 16(5) & (6) retrospective relief, Section 17(5) blocked credits, Supreme Court verdict in Safari Retreats, Rules 42 & 43 mathematical formulas, mandatory ISD regime under Section 20, and Rule 88A utilization order.

7.1 Foundational Architecture & Definitions

Input Tax Credit (ITC) prevents cascading by allowing tax paid on inward procurements to offset tax liability on outward supplies. Chapter V of the CGST Act [Sections 16 to 21] and Chapter V of the CGST Rules [Rules 36 to 45] establish the complete mechanism.

📌 Three Inward Buckets under GST:
• Inputs [Section 2(59)]: Any goods, other than capital goods, used or intended to be used by a supplier in the course or furtherance of business.
• Input Services [Section 2(60)]: Any service used or intended to be used by a supplier in the course or furtherance of business.
• Capital Goods [Section 2(19)]: Goods, the value of which is capitalised in the books of account of the person claiming the input tax credit and which are used or intended to be used in the course or furtherance of business.

Section 16 Eligibility & The 6 Statutory Conditions for Availing ITC

Under Section 16(1), every registered person shall be entitled to take credit of input tax charged on any supply of goods or services which are used or intended to be used in the course or furtherance of his business.

Under Section 16(2), no registered person shall be entitled to the credit of any input tax unless ALL the following 6 cumulative statutory conditions are fulfilled:

CONDITION 1 [Sec 16(2)(a)]

Possession of Valid Tax Document

Possession of tax invoice issued by supplier, debit note, bill of entry for imports, or ISD invoice under Rule 36(1).

CONDITION 2 [Sec 16(2)(aa)]

Communicated in Form GSTR-2B

Supplier must report the invoice/debit note in GSTR-1/IFF, and details must be reflected in recipient's GSTR-2B under Section 38.

CONDITION 3 [Sec 16(2)(b)]

Actual Receipt of Goods/Services

• Includes Deemed Delivery (Bill-to Ship-to model).
• Lots/Installments: ITC available ONLY upon receipt of the LAST LOT!

CONDITION 4 [Sec 16(2)(ba)]

Credit Not Restricted in GSTR-2B

The credit must not be restricted in GSTR-2B under Section 38 (e.g. newly registered supplier, continuous default in tax payment).

CONDITION 5 [Sec 16(2)(c)]

Tax Actually Paid to Government

The tax charged in respect of supply must have been actually paid to the Government, either in cash or through utilization of admissible ITC.

CONDITION 6 [Sec 16(2)(d)]

Furnishing of Return u/s 39

The recipient must have furnished the return under Section 39 (Form GSTR-3B) for the relevant tax period.

⚡ THE 180-DAY PAYMENT RULE [Second & Third Provisos to Section 16(2)]

Where a recipient fails to pay to the supplier the amount towards the value of supply along with tax payable thereon within 180 days from the date of issue of invoice:

• Reversal with Interest: An amount equal to the ITC availed must be paid by the recipient along with interest under Section 50(1) @ 18% p.a. (calculated from date of availing ITC till date of payment/reversal).
• Subsequent Re-availment: The recipient can re-avail the ITC in the month in which payment of value + tax is made!
• NO Time Limit for Re-availment: The time limit under Section 16(4) does NOT apply to re-availment of credit under the 180-day rule.
• Exceptions (Where 180-Day Rule Does NOT Apply):
  1. Supplies on which tax is payable under Reverse Charge Mechanism (RCM);
  2. Deemed supplies made without consideration under Schedule I;
  3. Value added under Section 15(2)(b) (expenses incurred by recipient on behalf of supplier).

🚫 Section 16(3) — Depreciation Bar on Capital Goods:
Where the registered person has claimed depreciation on the tax component of the cost of capital goods and plant and machinery under Section 32 of the Income-tax Act, 1961, the ITC on the said tax component SHALL NOT BE ALLOWED! (No double tax benefit!).

⏳ Section 16(4) — Statutory Time Limit for Availing ITC:
A registered person shall not be entitled to take ITC in respect of any invoice or debit note after:
• 30th November following the end of financial year to which such invoice or debit note pertains; OR
• The actual date of furnishing of the relevant Annual Return (Form GSTR-9),
whichever is EARLIER!

🆕 Section 16(5) & 16(6) Inserted by Finance (No. 2) Act, 2024 (Retrospective Relief)

• Section 16(5): For FY 2017-18, 2018-19, 2019-20 and 2020-21, a registered person is entitled to take ITC in any return filed under Section 39 up to 30th November, 2021.
• Section 16(6): Where registration of a person was cancelled and subsequently revoked, the time limit to avail ITC on invoices during cancellation period is extended up to 30 days from the date of order of revocation of cancellation.
(No refund of tax already paid or reversed shall be granted pursuant to this amendment).

Section 17 Apportionment of Credit & Banking Company Option

Where goods or services are used partly for business and partly for other purposes, or partly for taxable supplies and partly for exempt supplies:

Section Circumstance of Use Statutory ITC Treatment
Section 17(1) Partly for business purposes and partly for other (personal) purposes ITC attributable ONLY to business purposes is admissible.
Section 17(2) Partly for taxable (including zero-rated) supplies and partly for exempt supplies ITC attributable ONLY to taxable & zero-rated supplies is admissible. (Proportionate reversal under Rules 42 & 43).
Section 17(3) Scope of "Exempt Supply" for ITC Reversal Includes:
• Reverse charge supplies made by supplier;
• Transactions in securities (valued at 1% of sale value);
• Sale of land and sale of building (subject to Sch II 5(b)).
Excludes: Schedule III activities (High seas sales, merchanting trade, warehoused goods before clearance).
Section 17(4) Banking Company / Financial Institution / NBFC Option Two Options Every Financial Year:
1. Comply with general proportionate reversal under Rule 42 & 43; OR
2. Avail flat 50% of the eligible ITC on inputs, capital goods & input services each month, and the remaining 50% shall lapse!
Key Exam Rule: The 50% restriction does NOT apply to supplies between distinct persons (inter-branch) having same PAN!

Section 17(5) Blocked Credits — The Comprehensive Negative List

Notwithstanding anything contained in Section 16(1) and Section 18(1), ITC SHALL NOT BE AVAILABLE in respect of the following:

Clause Ineligible Supplies (Blocked Category) Exceptions (Where ITC is ALLOWED)
17(5)(a) Motor vehicles for transportation of persons having approved seating capacity of not more than 13 persons (including the driver). Allowed if used for:
1. Further supply of such motor vehicles;
2. Transportation of passengers (e.g. taxi, tour operator);
3. Imparting training on driving such motor vehicles.
(Note: Vehicles with capacity > 13 persons are NOT blocked!).
17(5)(aa) Vessels and Aircraft Allowed if used for:
1. Further supply of vessels/aircraft;
2. Transportation of passengers;
3. Imparting training (navigating/flying);
4. Transportation of goods.
17(5)(ab) General insurance, servicing, repair and maintenance of motor vehicles, vessels, or aircraft. Allowed where:
• The motor vehicle/vessel/aircraft itself is eligible u/s 17(5)(a)/(aa); OR
• Received by a manufacturer of such motor vehicle/vessel/aircraft; OR
• Received by a general insurance company in respect of insurance policy taken by it.
17(5)(b) • Food and beverages, outdoor catering, beauty treatment, health services, cosmetic and plastic surgery;
• Leasing, renting or hiring of motor vehicles, vessels or aircraft;
• Life insurance and health insurance;
• Membership of a club, health and fitness centre;
• Travel benefits extended to employees on vacation (LTC/home travel concession). 		Allowed ONLY IF:
1. Inward supply is used for making an outward taxable supply of the same category or as an element of a composite/mixed supply; OR
2. It is OBLIGATORY for an employer to provide the same to its employees under ANY LAW for the time being in force (e.g. mandatory canteen under Factories Act, mandatory health insurance during pandemic).
17(5)(c) Works contract services supplied for construction of an immovable property (other than plant and machinery). Allowed where:
• It is an input service for further supply of works contract service; OR
• Construction of Plant and Machinery!
17(5)(d) Goods or services received by a taxable person for construction of an immovable property on his own account (other than plant or machinery), even when used in course of business. • Construction of Plant and Machinery; OR
• Expenditure charged to revenue (repairs/renovations NOT capitalised!).
17(5)(e) Goods or services on which tax has been paid under Section 10 (Composition scheme). None (Strictly blocked).
17(5)(f) Goods or services received by a Non-Resident Taxable Person (NRTP). Allowed on goods imported by him under Section 17(5)(f).
17(5)(fa) Goods or services used or intended to be used for activities relating to Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) under Section 135 of Companies Act, 2013 [Inserted by FA 2023]. None (100% blocked statutory bar).
17(5)(g) Goods or services used for personal consumption. None.
17(5)(h) Goods lost, stolen, destroyed, written off, or disposed of by way of gift or free samples. None. (Full ITC reversal required).
17(5)(i) Any tax paid in terms of Section 74, 129 and 130 (tax evasion, detention, seizure, confiscation). None.

⚖️ Supreme Court Landmark Ruling: Safari Retreats Pvt. Ltd. (October 2024)

Issue: Can a builder/developer claim ITC on goods/services used in constructing a shopping mall or commercial building when the building is intended to be let out on rent (commercial leasing)?

Ruling of the Supreme Court:
• The Supreme Court affirmed the validity of Section 17(5)(d).
• The Court held that the expression "plant or machinery" in Section 17(5)(d) must be interpreted using the Functionality Test.
• If a building or immovable structure is constructed specifically as a functional tool/plant without which the commercial leasing service cannot be rendered, the High Court must examine whether it qualifies as a "plant" on a case-to-case factual inquiry.
• However, the statutory blockage on construction of immovable property capitalised in books remains constitutionally valid.

Section 18 ITC in Special Circumstances & Capital Goods Transfers

Section 18 governs special situations where a person becomes entitled to ITC, exits composition, transfers business, or ceases to be liable:

Section Special Event / Trigger Inward Stock Entitled to ITC
18(1)(a) Person applies for registration within 30 days of becoming liable to register Inputs held in stock, inputs contained in semi-finished/finished goods held in stock on the day immediately preceding the date from which he becomes liable to pay tax. (No capital goods credit).
18(1)(b) Person obtains Voluntary Registration under Section 25(3) Inputs held in stock, semi-finished/finished goods on the day immediately preceding the date of grant of registration. (No capital goods credit).
18(1)(c) Person ceases to pay tax under Composition Scheme and switches to regular scheme • Inputs held in stock, semi-finished/finished goods on day preceding date of liability to pay regular tax; AND
• Capital goods used for business, reduced by 5% per quarter or part thereof from the date of invoice.
18(1)(d) Exempt supply of goods/services becomes a Taxable Supply • Inputs in stock relatable to exempt supply; AND
• Capital goods exclusively used for such exempt supply, reduced by 5% per quarter or part thereof from date of invoice.
18(2) One-Year Cutoff Rule: A registered person shall NOT be entitled to take ITC under 18(1)(a)-(d) after the expiry of ONE YEAR from the date of issue of tax invoice!
18(3) Change in Constitution / Transfer of Business: On sale, merger, demerger, amalgamation, lease, or transfer of business with specific provision for transfer of liabilities, unutilized ITC is transferred to transferee via Form GST ITC-02. (In case of demerger, ITC apportioned in ratio of value of assets of new units).
18(4) Regular to Composition OR Taxable to Wholly Exempt: Pay an amount equal to ITC on inputs in stock + capital goods (computed on remaining useful life out of 60 months). Any balance credit in electronic credit ledger SHALL LAPSE!

⚡ SECTION 18(6) — SUPPLY OF CAPITAL GOODS ON WHICH ITC WAS AVAILED

In case of supply of capital goods or plant and machinery on which ITC has been taken, the registered person shall pay an amount equal to HIGHER OF THE FOLLOWING:

(A) ITC taken on said capital goods reduced by 5% per quarter or part thereof from the date of issue of invoice for capital goods; OR
(B) Tax payable on the Transaction Value of such capital goods determined under Section 15.

🔥 Crucial Proviso (Scrap Rule): Where refractory bricks, moulds, dies, jigs and fixtures are supplied as scrap, the taxable person may pay tax ONLY on the Transaction Value under Section 15 (the 5% per quarter reduction formula does NOT apply!).

Rules 42 & 43 Mathematical Models for Common Credit Reversal

📐 Rule 42: Inputs & Input Services

Let T = Total input tax on inputs & input services in the tax period.

Step 1: Ineligible Credits (Not Credited to Ledger):
• $T_1$ = Exclusively used for non-business purposes
• $T_2$ = Exclusively used for exempt supplies
• $T_3$ = Ineligible under Section 17(5)
• Eligible ITC Credited to Ledger: $C_1 = T - (T_1 + T_2 + T_3)$

Step 2: Segregate Exclusively Taxable:
• $T_4$ = Exclusively used for taxable & zero-rated supplies
• Common Credit: $C_2 = C_1 - T_4$

Step 3: Apportion Common Credit ($C_2$):
• Credit attributable to exempt supplies:
D_1 = (E / F) × C_2
(E = Exempt turnover, F = Total turnover of State)
• Credit attributable to non-business:
D_2 = 5% × C_2
• Eligible Common Credit: $C_3 = C_2 - (D_1 + D_2)$

Reversal: $D_1$ and $D_2$ are added to output tax liability in GSTR-3B.

📐 Rule 43: Capital Goods (60 Months Model)

Useful life of capital goods is statutorily fixed at 60 months (5 years) from date of invoice.

Step 1: Segregation of Capital Goods:
• Capital goods exclusively non-business / exempt: No credit.
• Capital goods exclusively taxable / zero-rated: 100% credited, no reversal.

Step 2: Common Capital Goods ($A$):
• Input tax on common capital goods denoted as $A$.
• Sum of all common capital goods in existence: $T_c = \sum A$
• Monthly credit attributable to common capital goods:
T_m = T_c / 60

Step 3: Monthly Ineligible Reversal:
• Let $T_r$ = Sum of $T_m$ for all common capital goods active in month.
• Credit attributable to exempt supplies:
T_e = (E / F) × T_r

Reversal: $T_e$ is added to output tax liability every month along with applicable interest if switching status.

Section 20 Input Service Distributor (ISD) — Mandatory Regime

Section 20 (substituted by Finance Act, 2024) makes it mandatory for an office of the supplier receiving tax invoices for input services for or on behalf of distinct persons to obtain registration as an Input Service Distributor (ISD).

📌 Core Rules of Credit Distribution by ISD:
• RCM Services: If Head Office receives services liable to RCM, HO must first pay tax under RCM in cash, and then distribute such credit as an ISD!
• Input Services Only: ISD can distribute credit on INPUT SERVICES ONLY. ISD cannot distribute credit on inputs or capital goods!
• Turnover Formula: Where input service is attributable to more than one recipient unit:
ITC Distributed to Unit = (Turnover of that Unit / Aggregate Turnover of all Recipient Units) × Common ITC
• Credit Nature:
  • CGST & SGST on intra-state service to intra-state unit → Distributed as CGST & SGST.
  • CGST & SGST on service distributed to inter-state unit → Distributed as IGST!

Rule 88A Manner & Sequence of ITC Utilization

Under Section 49(5), Section 49A, Section 49B and Rule 88A of the CGST Rules, ITC must be set off in the following strict hierarchy:

ITC Ledger Balance First Output Set-off Second Output Set-off Third Output Set-off
1. IGST Credit IGST Output Liability CGST or SGST/UTGST Output Liability in ANY ORDER and in ANY PROPORTION until IGST credit is 100% EXHAUSTED!
2. CGST Credit CGST Output Liability IGST Output Liability (if any) NEVER against SGST
3. SGST / UTGST Credit SGST / UTGST Output IGST Output Liability (if any) NEVER against CGST
⚠️ Golden Exam Principle: Credit of CGST can NEVER be used to pay SGST, and credit of SGST can NEVER be used to pay CGST! Furthermore, neither CGST nor SGST credit can be touched until the IGST credit balance is completely zero!

TYK Master Cases ICAI Test Your Knowledge — Solved Practical Problems (1 to 29)

🎯 TYK Question 1 • Flamingo Ltd. (Airline Passenger & Catering)

Facts: Flamingo Ltd. operates passenger flights. Inward procurements: (i) Aircraft for passenger transport (seats > 13); (ii) In-flight food & beverages served free to passengers; (iii) Outdoor catering for staff training seminar. Determine ITC eligibility.

Statutory Analysis:
1. Aircraft: Seating capacity > 13 and used for transportation of passengers → Excluded from blockage under Section 17(5)(aa). ITC ALLOWED.
2. In-flight Catering: Supplied as part of composite supply of passenger transport → ITC ALLOWED under Section 17(5)(b)(i).
3. Outdoor Catering for Seminar: Not an outward supply of same category nor mandatory by law → BLOCKED u/s 17(5)(b)(i).

🎯 TYK Question 2 • Jumbo Sales (180-Day Supplier Payment Rule)

Facts: Jumbo Sales purchased goods on 1st April (invoice ₹10,00,000 + ₹1,80,000 GST) and availed full ITC in April return. Jumbo Sales paid ₹6,00,000 + tax on 15th July, but failed to pay balance ₹4,00,000 within 180 days (which ended 28th September). On 10th December, Jumbo Sales paid the remaining ₹4,00,000. Examine tax and interest implications.

Solution:
• Under the second proviso to Section 16(2), on proportionate unpaid amount (₹4,00,000 → Tax ₹72,000), ITC of ₹72,000 must be reversed in the return for month after 180 days with interest @ 18% p.a. from date of availing ITC till date of reversal.
• Upon payment of balance ₹4,00,000 on 10th December, Jumbo Sales can re-avail ₹72,000 ITC in December return without any interest or time limit barrier!

🎯 TYK Question 3 • Garment Factory (Goods Lost by Fire)

Facts: Garment manufacturer bought fabric worth ₹5,00,000 + 5% GST. Fabric worth ₹1,00,000 was completely destroyed by accidental fire before being put into production. Can ITC be claimed on the destroyed fabric?

Solution: Under Section 17(5)(h), goods lost, stolen, destroyed, written off or disposed of by way of gift or free samples are blocked from ITC. Hence, ITC of ₹5,000 on destroyed fabric is INADMISSIBLE (must be reversed). ITC on remaining ₹4,00,000 is allowed.

🎯 TYK Question 4 • Ceramity Ltd. (Works Contract for Factory vs. Office Building)

Facts: Ceramity Ltd. incurred works contract charges: (a) Construction of factory foundation for installing heavy kiln machinery (capitalised under Plant & Machinery); (b) Construction of administrative office building; (c) Pipeline laid outside factory premises connecting river. Determine ITC.

Solution:
• (a) Foundation for heavy kiln qualifies as Plant and Machinery (foundation and structural support) → ITC ALLOWED under Section 17(5)(c).
• (b) Administrative office building is immovable property other than P&M → BLOCKED u/s 17(5)(c).
• (c) Pipelines laid outside factory premises are specifically excluded from definition of Plant and Machinery under Explanation to Section 17 → BLOCKED u/s 17(5)(c).

🎯 TYK Question 5 • Car Dealer (Demo Cars vs. Staff Transportation)

Facts: Krishna Motors, an authorised car dealer, purchased: (a) 5 cars for resale; (b) 2 demo cars used for giving test drives to potential buyers; (c) 1 car (7-seater) used for daily commute of Managing Director. Determine ITC.

Solution:
• (a) Cars for resale: Further supply of vehicles → ITC ALLOWED u/s 17(5)(a)(A).
• (b) Demo cars: Capitalised as demo vehicles but used to promote sales (further supply) → ITC ALLOWED (Circular 231/2024 clarifies demo vehicles qualify for ITC if supplier is dealer of motor vehicles).
• (c) MD's car: Seating capacity ≤ 13, personal/staff commute → STRICTLY BLOCKED u/s 17(5)(a).

🎯 TYK Question 6 • Voluntary Registration Stock Credit [Sec 18(1)(b)]

Facts: Mr. X applied for voluntary registration on 1st June, registration granted on 15th June. Details of stock on 14th June: Inputs in stock invoice dated 10th May (within 1 yr) ₹2,00,000 (tax ₹36,000); Inputs in stock invoice dated 1st January (preceding year - 17 months old) ₹50,000 (tax ₹9,000); Capital goods purchased on 1st March ₹5,00,000 (tax ₹90,000). Determine eligible ITC.

Solution: Under Section 18(1)(b):
1. Inputs in stock on 14th June with invoice within 1 year → Eligible = ₹36,000.
2. Inputs older than 1 year under Section 18(2) → Ineligible = ₹0.
3. Capital goods are NOT eligible under voluntary registration Section 18(1)(b) → Ineligible = ₹0.
Total Eligible ITC = ₹36,000.

🎯 TYK Question 7 • Disposal of Capital Goods [Section 18(6)]

Facts: ABC Ltd. purchased machinery on 1st April 2023 for ₹20,00,000 + 18% GST (ITC availed ₹3,60,000). On 20th November 2024, the machinery is sold to an unrelated buyer for ₹12,00,000. Determine amount payable u/s 18(6) (GST rate 18%).

Statutory Computation: Under Section 18(6), compare:
• Method 1 (ITC reduced by 5% per quarter):
  Quarters elapsed: Q1 2023-24 (1), Q2 (2), Q3 (3), Q4 (4), Q1 2024-25 (5), Q2 (6), Q3 (7 - part of quarter) = 7 quarters.
  Reduction = 7 × 5% = 35%.
  Remaining amount = ₹3,60,000 − (35% of ₹3,60,000) = ₹3,60,000 − ₹1,26,000 = ₹2,34,000.
• Method 2 (Tax on Transaction Value):
  ₹12,00,000 × 18% = ₹2,16,000.
Amount Payable u/s 18(6): HIGHER of above = ₹2,34,000!

🎯 TYK Question 8 • Banking Company 50% Rule [Section 17(4)]

Facts: National Bank of Commerce availed option u/s 17(4). In August: (i) Eligible ITC on inward supplies from vendors = ₹40,00,000; (ii) Ineligible ITC u/s 17(5) on motor cars = ₹6,00,000; (iii) Inter-branch inward supplies from separate registered unit in Mumbai = ₹10,00,000. Compute net ITC credited.

Solution:
• Ineligible u/s 17(5) is strictly excluded first → ₹6,00,000 is 100% barred.
• Third-party eligible credit: 50% of ₹40,00,000 = ₹20,00,000 (remaining ₹20L lapses).
• Distinct person (inter-branch) credit: 50% restriction does NOT apply → 100% of ₹10,00,000 = ₹10,00,000!
Total Admissible ITC = ₹20,00,000 + ₹10,00,000 = ₹30,00,000.

📚 Key Takeaways from Remaining ICAI Practical Cases (TYK 9 to 29):

  • Rule 42 Common Reversal: If exempt supplies are ₹20L and total turnover is ₹100L ($E/F = 20\%$), exactly 20% of common pool $C_2$ must be reversed ($D_1$) plus 5% non-business ($D_2$).
  • Rule 43 Capital Goods: When a machine previously used exclusively for exempt supplies is brought into common use, it is converted to $A$, and credit is availed with monthly reversal $T_e = (E/F) \times (A/60)$.
  • Rule 88A Exhaustion: If IGST output is ₹50k and IGST input is ₹1,00,000, remaining ₹50k IGST credit CAN be used against CGST or SGST in any ratio (e.g. ₹25k each or ₹50k CGST) before utilizing CGST/SGST credit.
  • CSR Expenditure: Post-amendment by Finance Act 2023, Section 17(5)(fa) explicitly blocks CSR expenses even if mandated by Companies Act.
  • Free Samples / Gifts: No ITC allowed on inputs used in free sample distribution. If ITC was already availed, it must be reversed under Section 17(5)(h).
