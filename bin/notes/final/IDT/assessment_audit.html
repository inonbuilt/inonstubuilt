Note 1: Procedure & Forms for Provisional Assessment (Sec 60 & Rule 98)

Step-by-step statutory execution from initial application to final assessment and bond release:

Form GST ASMT-01
Application by Taxable Person
Submitted electronically requesting payment of tax on a provisional basis indicating reasons for inability to determine value/rate.
Form GST ASMT-02
Notice for Information / Documents
Proper Officer issues notice within 15 days seeking additional documents or information from the applicant.
Form GST ASMT-03
Reply to Notice
Taxable person furnishes reply and requested documents within 15 days of notice receipt.
Form GST ASMT-04
Provisional Assessment Order (PAO)
Officer issues order within 90 days from ASMT-01 allowing payment on provisional basis at specified rate/value.
Form GST ASMT-05
Execution of Bond with Security
Taxable person executes bond with security (bank guarantee up to 25% of bond amount) binding payment of differential tax.
Form GST ASMT-06
Notice for Final Assessment
Proper Officer issues notice calling for information/records required for finalizing the assessment.
Form GST ASMT-07
Final Assessment Order (FAO)
Issued within 6 months from PAO. Extendable by Joint/Addl. Comm. by 6 months, and by Commissioner up to 4 years (Total max: 5 Years).
Form GST ASMT-08
Application for Release of Security
Taxable person applies for release of security/bond after payment of differential tax dues as finalized in ASMT-07.
Form GST ASMT-09
Order for Release of Security
Proper Officer passes order releasing bond and security within 7 working days of receipt of application.

Note 2: Return Filing & Order Deemed Withdrawal under Section 62 (Best Judgment)

If a non-filer files a valid return within 60 days from service of assessment order in ASMT-13, the order is deemed to have been withdrawn. However, liability to pay interest u/s 50(1) and late fee u/s 47 continues.

2. Audit Under GST (Section 65 vs. Section 66)

The CGST Act provides two statutory audit frameworks to verify compliance, examine records, and ensure revenue integrity:

Departmental Audit Section 65
  • Conducted By: Proper Officer (Commissioner or officer authorized by him by general or special order).
  • Where Conducted: Place of business of registered person OR in office of the tax authority.
  • Prior Notice: Notice in Form GST ADT-01 issued at least 15 working days prior to commencement.
  • Time Limit for Completion: Within 3 months from date of commencement; extendable by Commissioner by further period not exceeding 6 months (Total: 9 Months).
  • Submission of Report: Proper Officer informs registered person of findings, grounds, rights & obligations in Form GST ADT-02 within 30 days of completion.
  • Audit Expenses: Entirely borne by the GST Department.
  • Scope of Audit: Books of accounts, turnover, exemptions, deductions, rate of tax, input tax credit availed/utilized, and payment of taxes.
Special Audit Section 66
  • Directed By: Assistant / Deputy Commissioner with prior approval of the Commissioner.
  • Conducted By: A Chartered Accountant (CA) or Cost Accountant (CMA) nominated by the Commissioner.
  • Statutory Trigger: At any stage of scrutiny, inquiry, or investigation, if officer is of opinion that: (i) Value not correctly declared, OR (ii) ITC availed not within normal limits.
  • Direction Notice: Taxable person directed in Form GST ADT-03 to get records audited.
  • Time Limit: CA/CMA submits report within 90 days; extendable on application by further 90 days (Total: 180 Days).
  • Audit Expenses & Fee: Determined and paid entirely by the Commissioner (not payable by registered person).
  • Principles of Natural Justice: Taxable person given opportunity of being heard if any audit material is used in proceedings u/s 73/74/74A.

3. Liability to Pay Tax in Special Cases (Sections 85 to 91)

Statutory rules determining joint, several, and individual tax liabilities in business restructuring, agency, corporate insolvency, and partnership succession:

Section 85
Transfer of Business (Whole or Part)
Dues Before Transfer: Transferor and transferee are jointly and severally liable to pay tax, interest, or penalty determined before or after transfer.
Dues After Transfer: Transferee is exclusively liable as if he had carried on the business.
Section 86
Principal and Agent
Where an agent supplies or receives any taxable goods on behalf of his principal, such agent and principal are jointly and severally liable for the tax payable on such goods.
Section 87
Amalgamation or Merger of Companies
Two or more companies amalgamating under High Court / NCLT order are treated as distinct companies until date of order. Transactions between them before order date are taxable supplies, and registration continues until order date.
Section 89
Liability of Directors of Private Company
Private Company: Directors during the period tax dues relate are jointly and severally liable, unless a director proves non-recovery was not due to gross neglect, misfeasance, or breach of duty.
Public Company: Directors are NOT personally liable for company dues.
Section 90
Partners of a Firm & Retirement
Firm & Partners: Partners are jointly & severally liable for all dues.
Retirement: Retiring partner must intimate retirement to Commissioner in writing within 30 days. If not intimated, liability continues till date of intimation.
Deceased Partner: Legal representative liable only to extent of estate received from deceased partner.
Section 91
Guardians, Trustees & Agents
Where business of minor, incapacitated person, or trust is conducted by guardian, trustee, or agent, the tax, interest, or penalty is levied upon and recoverable from such guardian/trustee to same extent as from minor/beneficiary.
