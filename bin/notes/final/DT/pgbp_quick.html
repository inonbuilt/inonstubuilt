Closing WDV Algorithm:
$ ext{Opening WDV} + ext{Actual Additions during year} - ext{Moneys Payable (Sales/Insurance)} = ext{WDV for Depreciation}$.
• If Moneys Payable $>$ (Opening WDV + Additions), WDV becomes NIL and excess is Short Term Capital Gain u/s 50.
• If all assets in block are sold but balance remains in block, WDV becomes NIL and balance is Short Term Capital Loss u/s 50.

3. Section 43B & Clause (h) MSME Mandate

Deduction allowable only on actual payment on or before the due date of filing return u/s 139(1), EXCEPT for clause (h):

  • Statutory liabilities: Taxes, duties, cesses, cess, GST, employee PF/ESI contributions.
  • Financing costs: Interest to Scheduled Banks, Co-operative Banks, Financial Institutions, NBFCs.
  • Bonus & Commission: Payable to employees.
  • Leave Encashment: Payable to employees.
CRITICAL EXAM ALERT — Section 43B(h) for MSMEs:
Any sum payable to a Micro or Small enterprise beyond the time limit specified in Section 15 of MSMED Act (15 days if no agreement; max 45 days if written agreement) is allowed ONLY in the year of actual payment! The benefit of payment up to return filing due date u/s 139(1) is NOT AVAILABLE for clause (h)!

5. High-Frequency Exam Traps

Trap 1: Cash payment split into invoices!
Sec 40A(3) applies to aggregate payments made to a person in a single day. If two bills of ₹ 6,000 each are paid in cash to the same person on the same day (total ₹ 12,000), 100% is disallowed!
Trap 2: Additional Depreciation under Sec 115BAC!
Assessees opting for the Default Tax Regime u/s 115BAC / Sec 202 CANNOT CLAIM additional depreciation u/s 32(1)(iia) or deductions u/s 35AD / 33AB.
