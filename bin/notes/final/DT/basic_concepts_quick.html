1. Tax Rates & Regime Slabs (Tax Year 2026-27 / AY 2027-28)

Under the Income-tax Act, 2025, the Default Tax Regime (Section 202 / Old 115BAC) applies automatically to all Individuals, HUFs, AOPs, BOIs, and AJPs unless an affirmative opt-out declaration is exercised.

Total Income Slab Default Tax Regime (Sec 202) Rate Normal / Optional Regime Rate Statutory Remarks
Up to ₹ 3,00,000 Nil Nil (₹ 2.5L / ₹ 3L sr cit / ₹ 5L super sr cit) Universal basic exemption under Default Regime.
₹ 3,00,001 to ₹ 7,00,000 5% 5% (₹ 2.5L / ₹ 3L to ₹ 5L) Tax on ₹ 7L under Default Regime = ₹ 20,000.
₹ 7,00,001 to ₹ 10,00,000 10% 20% (₹ 5,00,001 to ₹ 10,00,000) Tax on ₹ 10L under Default Regime = ₹ 50,000.
₹ 10,00,001 to ₹ 12,00,000 15% 30% (Exceeding ₹ 10,00,000) Tax on ₹ 12L under Default Regime = ₹ 80,000.
₹ 12,00,001 to ₹ 15,00,000 20% 30% Tax on ₹ 15L under Default Regime = ₹ 1,40,000.
Above ₹ 15,00,000 30% 30% Standard maximum slab rate.
Rebate u/s 156 (Old Section 87A) Fast Formula:
• Default Regime: If Total Income $\le ₹ 7,00,000$, Rebate = $100\%$ of Tax or $₹ 25,000$, whichever is lower (Tax payable = NIL).
• Marginal Relief on Rebate: If Total Income slightly exceeds $₹ 7,00,000$, Tax Payable cannot exceed $( ext{Total Income} - ₹ 7,00,000)$.
• Normal Regime: If Total Income $\le ₹ 5,00,000$, Rebate = $100\%$ of Tax or $₹ 12,500$, whichever is lower.

2. Surcharge Architecture & Marginal Relief Steps

Total Income Range Default Regime (Sec 202) Normal Regime Special Incomes (Sec 111A, 112, 112A, Dividend)
₹ 50 Lakhs to ₹ 1 Crore 10% 10% 10% maximum
₹ 1 Crore to ₹ 2 Crores 15% 15% 15% maximum
₹ 2 Crores to ₹ 5 Crores 25% 25% Capped at 15%
Above ₹ 5 Crores Capped at 25% 37% Capped at 15%
3-Step Marginal Relief Algorithm:
1. Calculate Tax + Surcharge on Total Income ($T_1$).
2. Calculate Tax on Threshold Limit (₹ 50L / 1 Cr / 2 Cr / 5 Cr) with lower surcharge ($T_2$).
3. Marginal Relief = $\maxig(0,\; T_1 - [T_2 + ( ext{Total Income} - ext{Threshold})]ig)$.

5. High-Frequency Exam Traps & Pitfalls

Trap 1: Rebate u/s 87A / 156 on Special Rate Incomes!
Rebate is NOT available against tax payable u/s 112A (LTCG on equity shares) exceeding ₹ 1,25,000 as per statutory proviso. However, it is allowable against normal STCG/LTCG u/s 112 under standard provisions unless specifically restricted.
Trap 2: Health & Education Cess Sequencing!
HEC @ 4% is ALWAYS computed on the total of (Tax + Surcharge - Marginal Relief). It is NEVER computed on gross tax before marginal relief.
Trap 3: Surcharge on Firm vs Company vs Individual!
• Partnership Firm / LLP: Flat 12% surcharge if Total Income > ₹ 1 Crore.
• Domestic Company: 7% (> ₹ 1 Cr) and 12% (> ₹ 10 Cr); under Sec 115BAA/BAB: flat 10%.
• Foreign Company: 2% (> ₹ 1 Cr) and 5% (> ₹ 10 Cr).
