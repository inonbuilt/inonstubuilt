1. Minimum Alternate Tax (MAT Section 206 / 115JB)

Applicable to all companies if Income-tax payable on Total Income is less than 15% of Book Profit (9% for units in IFSC).

Additions to Net Profit (if debited to P&L) Deductions from Net Profit (if credited to P&L)
• Income-tax paid/payable, surcharge, HEC, dividend distribution tax.
• Amounts transferred to any reserve.
• Provisions for un-ascertained liabilities.
• Provision for losses of subsidiary companies.
• Dividends paid or proposed.
• Expenditure relatable to exempt income u/s 10 (excl. 10AA).
• Depreciation debited to P&L.		 • Amount withdrawn from any reserve or provision.
• Incomes exempt u/s 10 (excl. 10AA).
• Depreciation debited to P&L excluding depreciation on revaluation of assets.
• Amount withdrawn from revaluation reserve (to extent of extra depreciation).
• Brought forward loss OR unabsorbed depreciation as per books, whichever is lower.
• Profits of sick industrial company.
MAT Credit Rules (Section 115JAA):
$ ext{MAT Credit} = ext{Tax paid under MAT} - ext{Tax payable under normal provisions}$.
• Can be carried forward for 15 Assessment Years.
• Set-off in future year allowed up to difference between Normal Tax and MAT.
• MAT does NOT APPLY to companies opting for Section 115BAA (22%) or Section 115BAB (15%).

2. Alternate Minimum Tax (AMT Section 227 / 115JC)

Applicable to all non-corporate assessees claiming deduction under Chapter VI-A (Part C - profit linked), Section 10AA, or Section 35AD, where Adjusted Total Income > ₹ 20 Lakhs.

AMT Calculation Steps:
$ ext{Adjusted Total Income (ATI)} = ext{Total Income} + ext{Deductions u/s 10AA} + ext{Deductions under VI-A (Part C)} + ext{Deductions u/s 35AD (less depr u/s 32)}$.
$ ext{AMT Payable} = \mathbf{18.5\%} imes ext{Adjusted Total Income (ATI)}$ (9% for units in IFSC) + Surcharge + 4% HEC.
• AMT does NOT APPLY to assessees opting for the Default Tax Regime u/s 115BAC / Sec 202.

3. Partnership Firms & LLPs: Section 40(b) Partner Remuneration

Book Profit Slab Maximum Permissible Remuneration u/s 40(b)
On first ₹ 3,00,000 of book profit, OR in case of loss ₹ 1,50,000 OR 90% of Book Profit, whichever is MORE.
On the balance of Book Profit 60% of Book Profit.
Interest on Capital to Partners:
• Must be authorized by the Partnership Deed.
• Relates to period after deed execution.
• Capped at simple interest of 12% per annum. Any excess interest is disallowed in firm's hands!

5. High-Yield Exam Traps

Trap 1: Firm Remuneration must be to WORKING partners only!
Deduction u/s 40(b) is allowed ONLY for remuneration paid to working partners. Any salary/remuneration paid to a non-working/sleeping partner is 100% disallowed!
Trap 2: Partner's Share of Profit is Exempt!
The share of profit of a partner from the firm is 100% EXEMPT in the partner's hands u/s 10(2A). Only interest (up to 12%) and remuneration (up to 40(b) limit) are taxable as PGBP in partner's hands!
