1. Legal Matrix: Planning vs Avoidance vs Evasion

Parameter Tax Planning Tax Avoidance Tax Evasion
Legal Status 100% legal, within the letter and spirit of law. Within legal letter, but defeats statutory intent / spirit. Completely illegal, criminal, fraudulent.
Methods Claiming statutory deductions (80JJAA, 54, 32). Colorable devices, treaty shopping, round-tripping. Suppression of sales, fake invoices, undisclosed cash.
Remedy / Action Encouraged by government. Invokes GAAR / SAAR to recharacterize transactions. Penalties (Sec 270A/271AAD) & Criminal Prosecution.

2. General Anti-Avoidance Rule (GAAR - Sections 95 to 102)

Under Section 95, an arrangement entered into by an assessee may be declared to be an Impermissible Avoidance Arrangement (IAA), and the consequences in relation to tax can be determined (disregarding steps, recharacterizing debt into equity, denying treaty benefits).

The Dual Test for Impermissible Avoidance Arrangement (IAA):
$ ext{Arrangement is IAA} \iff ( ext{Main Purpose is Tax Benefit}) \;\mathbf{AND}\; ( ext{Presence of at least ONE Tainted Element})$.

3. The 4 Statutory Tainted Elements (Sec 96(1))

Tainted Element Statutory Scope / Benchmark
1. Non-Arm's Length dealings Arrangement creates rights or obligations which would not normally be created between persons dealing at arm's length.
2. Misuse or Abuse of Law Results directly or indirectly in the misuse or abuse of the provisions of the Income-tax Act.
3. Lack of Commercial Substance Arrangement lacks commercial substance or is deemed to lack commercial substance in whole or in part u/s 97.
4. Non-Bona Fide Purpose Arrangement is entered into or carried out by means, or in a manner, which is not normally employed for bona fide business purposes.

4. Deemed Lack of Commercial Substance (Section 97)

An arrangement is deemed to lack commercial substance if:

  • The substance or effect of the arrangement as a whole is inconsistent with, or differs significantly from, the form of its individual steps.
  • It involves Round-trip financing (funds transferred among parties with no substantial commercial objective other than tax benefit).
  • It involves an Accommodating party or an entity without significant business purpose.
  • It includes elements that have the effect of offsetting or cancelling each other.
  • It disguises the value, location, source, ownership, or control of funds.

5. GAAR Safe Harbors & Key Traps

Statutory Exceptions / Safe Harbors (Rule 10U):
1. Monetary Ceiling: GAAR does NOT apply to an arrangement where the tax benefit arising in the relevant financial year in aggregate to all parties does NOT EXCEED ₹ 3 Crores.
2. Foreign Portfolio Investors (FPIs): FPIs who do not take treaty benefits are exempt.
3. Grandfathering: Investments made prior to 1st April 2017 are protected.
Approving Panel Safeguard (Sec 144BA): If assessee objects to AO's reference, the matter goes to the Approving Panel (headed by a retired High Court Judge + 2 Pr. CITs). The Approving Panel's directions are binding on the tax authority!
