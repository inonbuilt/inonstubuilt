4. Interest Framework (Sections 234A, 234B & 234C)

Section Default Event Rate & Period of Interest
Sec 234A Delay in filing Return of Income beyond 139(1) due date 1% per month (or part of month) on assessed tax less advance tax/TDS, from day after due date to date of filing.
Sec 234B Failure to pay Advance Tax or advance tax paid < 90% of assessed tax 1% per month from 1st April of AY to date of determination of income u/s 143(1) or regular assessment.
Sec 234C Deferment / Shortfall in payment of individual advance tax instalments 1% per month for 3 months on shortfall below 12% (June), 36% (Sept), 75% (Dec); and 1% for 1 month on shortfall below 100% (March).

5. High-Frequency Exam Traps

Trap 1: Sec 194Q vs Sec 206C(1H) Conflict Hierarchy!
If a transaction attracts both Section 194Q (buyer TDS) and Section 206C(1H) (seller TCS), Section 194Q PREVAILS! The buyer must deduct TDS @ 0.1%, and seller will NOT collect TCS!
Trap 2: Partner Remuneration TDS u/s 194T!
Under Section 194T, the firm must deduct TDS @ 10% on salary, remuneration, commission, or interest paid to a partner if aggregate exceeds ₹ 20,000 in a FY!
