1. Statutory Return Filing Due Dates (Section 139(1))

Category of Assessee Prescribed Due Date
Company Assessee (Domestic or Foreign) 31st October of the Assessment Year.
Non-Corporate Assessee whose accounts are required to be audited u/s 44AB or any other law 31st October of the Assessment Year.
Partner of a Firm whose accounts are required to be audited 31st October of the Assessment Year.
Assessee required to furnish Transfer Pricing Report u/s 92E 30th November of the Assessment Year.
All Other Assessees (Individuals, non-audit HUFs/firms) 31st July of the Assessment Year.

2. Belated (139(4)), Revised (139(5)) & Updated Returns (139(8A))

Return Type Time Limit for Filing Additional Tax / Penalty
Belated Return (Sec 139(4)) Up to 31st December of AY (or completion of assessment, whichever is earlier). Fee u/s 234F: ₹ 5,000 (₹ 1,000 if total income $\le$ ₹ 5 Lakhs). Loss carry forward denied.
Revised Return (Sec 139(5)) Up to 31st December of AY (or completion of assessment). Can revise original or belated return. No fee. Replaces original return completely.
Updated Return (Sec 139(8A)) Within 24 Months from end of the relevant Assessment Year. • Within 12 months: Additional Tax = 25% of (Tax + Interest).
• Between 12 to 24 months: Additional Tax = 50% of (Tax + Interest).
Restrictions on Updated Return u/s 139(8A): Cannot be filed if: (a) it is a loss return, (b) it decreases tax liability or increases refund, (c) search u/s 132 or survey u/s 133A initiated, or (d) prosecution proceedings initiated!

3. Assessment Regimes Summary

Section Assessment Head Statutory Notice & Limitation Period
Sec 143(1) Summary Intimation / Processing Within 9 months from the end of the financial year in which return is furnished.
Sec 143(2) Notice for Regular Scrutiny Assessment Within 3 months from the end of the financial year in which return is furnished.
Sec 143(3) Scrutiny Assessment Order Within 12 months from the end of the relevant Assessment Year.
Sec 144 Best Judgment Assessment Triggered on failure to file return u/s 139, failure to comply with 142(1) or 143(2) notice.

4. Reassessment Framework (Sections 147 to 151)

Statutory Stage Mandatory Legal Requirement
Section 148A Inquiry AO must conduct inquiry, grant show-cause opportunity (7 to 30 days) to assessee, and pass order u/s 148A(d) with prior specified authority approval.
Time Limit: Standard Case Notice u/s 148 can be issued up to 3 years from the end of the relevant AY.
Time Limit: Serious Tax Evasion Can be issued beyond 3 years up to 5 years from end of AY ONLY IF escaped income represented in the form of asset, expenditure, or entry exceeds ₹ 50 Lakhs.

5. High-Frequency Exam Traps

Trap 1: Notice u/s 143(2) served after 3 months!
Service of notice u/s 143(2) within 3 months from the end of the financial year in which return is filed is a mandatory jurisdictional condition. An assessment completed pursuant to an invalid/delayed notice is void ab initio!
Trap 2: Updated return filing when search is initiated!
Where a search is initiated u/s 132 or survey u/s 133A, assessee CANNOT file an updated return for that year and any preceding 2 assessment years!
