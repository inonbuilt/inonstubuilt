1. Cash Transaction Restrictions Matrix

Section Prohibited Transaction Monetary Threshold Permissible Modes
Sec 269SS Acceptance of any Loan, Deposit, or Specified Advance Aggregate ≥ ₹ 20,000 (including unpaid balance) A/c Payee Cheque, A/c Payee Draft, ECS, or electronic banking. (₹ 2 Lakhs for Primary Agri Credit Society).
Sec 269T Repayment of any Loan, Deposit, or Specified Advance Aggregate ≥ ₹ 20,000 (including interest) A/c Payee Cheque, A/c Payee Draft, ECS, or electronic banking.
Sec 269ST Receipt of an amount in cash ≥ ₹ 2,00,000:
(a) in aggregate from a person in a day, OR
(b) in respect of a single transaction, OR
(c) in respect of transactions relating to one event/occasion.		 A/c Payee Cheque, Draft, or ECS only.

3. Strict 100% Penalties for Cash Violations

Violation Section Penalty Section Penalty Quantum Adjudicating Authority
Violation of Section 269SSSection 271D100% of the loan / deposit accepted.Joint Commissioner of Income Tax.
Violation of Section 269TSection 271E100% of the loan / deposit repaid.Joint Commissioner of Income Tax.
Violation of Section 269STSection 271DA100% of the cash received.Joint Commissioner of Income Tax.
Reasonable Cause Exception (Sec 273B): No penalty is imposable u/s 271D, 271E, or 271DA if the assessee proves that there was a reasonable cause for the failure (e.g. emergency medical payment on bank holiday).

4. Higher TDS / TCS for Non-Filers (Sec 206AB & 206CCA)

Where tax is required to be deducted/collected on payments to a "Specified Person" (who has not filed return for preceding previous year and aggregate TDS/TCS $\ge ₹ 50,000$):
$ ext{TDS / TCS Rate} = ext{HIGHER of:}$
(a) Twice the rate specified in the relevant provision, OR
(b) Twice the rate or rates in force, OR
(c) 5%.

5. High-Frequency Exam Traps

Trap 1: Bearer cheque is treated as CASH!
Accepting or repaying a loan via Bearer Cheque or Crossed Cheque (not Account Payee) is an absolute violation of Section 269SS / 269T and attracts a 100% penalty!
Trap 2: Splitting single cash receipt across multiple days!
Section 269ST prohibits cash receipt of ₹ 2 Lakhs or more in respect of transactions relating to ONE EVENT OR OCCASION. Receiving ₹ 50,000 daily for 5 days for a wedding event is an outright violation!
