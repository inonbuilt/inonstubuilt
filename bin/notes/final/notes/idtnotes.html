CA Final Indirect Tax Laws (Paper 5, 100 Marks) evaluates comprehensive technical competence across two integrated disciplines: Part I: Goods and Services Tax (80 Marks) and Part II: Customs Law & Foreign Trade Policy (20 Marks) under the ICAI New Scheme.

Calcium Notes delivers 100% free, high-yield, chapter-wise revision notes across all 32 chapters. Our notes systematically integrate statutory provisions (CGST, IGST, UTGST, and Customs Acts), high-frequency CBIC circulars (Del Credere Agents, Agreeing to Obligation/Liquidated Damages under Circular 178/10/2022, Motor Vehicle Salvage Circular 215/09/2024, and Tenancy Rights Circular 44/18/2018), and step-by-step solutions to ICAI case scenarios specifically crafted for the May 2027 and November 2026/2027 examination attempts.
