About CA Final Advanced Auditing, Assurance & Professional Ethics (Paper 3)

Under the ICAI New Scheme of Education and Training, Paper 3 tests conceptual mastery across Standards on Auditing (SAs), statutory auditor duties under Section 143 of the Companies Act, 2013, Form ADT-4 fraud reporting, and reporting under CARO 2020. Calcium Notes provides structured, exam-oriented revision summaries, flowchart breakdowns, and clause-by-clause comparative tables to help CA Final candidates achieve high retention and examination success.
