About CA Final Advanced Financial Management (Paper 2)

Under the ICAI New Scheme of Education and Training, Paper 2: Advanced Financial Management (AFM) evaluates candidates' capability to formulate financial strategy, optimize capital allocation, manage global and enterprise-level financial risks, and value complex businesses and derivative instruments. Calcium Notes delivers rigorously organized, exam-focused chapter revision notes, step-by-step practical illustration walkthroughs, formula cheat-sheets, and conceptual summaries designed specifically for CA Final aspirants.
