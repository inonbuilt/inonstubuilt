About CA Final Direct Tax Laws & International Taxation (Paper 4)

CA Final Direct Tax Laws and International Taxation evaluates deep statutory comprehension of the Income-tax Act, 1961, as amended by the latest Finance Acts. It covers assessment of entities, withholding tax provisions (TDS & TCS), Transfer Pricing computations, Base Erosion and Profit Shifting (BEPS) measures, and DTAA interpretations. Calcium Notes provides master reference charts, threshold filters, and judicial analysis to empower students for their examination preparation.
