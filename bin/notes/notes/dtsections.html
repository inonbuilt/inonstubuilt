About the CA Final Direct Tax Section-Wise Directory

This section-by-section master directory is extracted chapter-by-chapter directly from the official ICAI Study Material (Modules 1 to 4) for CA Final Paper 4: Direct Tax Laws & International Taxation. Arranged in ascending numerical order from Section 1 up to Section 536, it provides students with a rapid-access reference for statutory sections, particulars, and chapter contexts across substantive tax provisions, deductions, entity assessments, withholding taxes (TDS/TCS), assessment procedures, appellate mechanisms, and international taxation.
