About CA Final Indirect Tax Laws (Paper 5)

CA Final Indirect Tax Laws encompasses the Central Goods and Services Tax (CGST) Act, Integrated GST (IGST) Act, Customs Act, 1962, and the Foreign Trade Policy. Calcium Notes brings practical clarity to intricate procedural topics including Provisional Assessments (ASMT-01 to 09), Scrutiny, Best Judgment Orders, Departmental and Special Audits, and third-party tax liabilities.
